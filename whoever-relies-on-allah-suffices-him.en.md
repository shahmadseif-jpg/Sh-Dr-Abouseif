Demonstrations in the streets of France, and economic crises shaking the old continent, and inflation figures announced by the Federal Reserve that rise and fall while people do not know what lies behind them. And if the circle widens you see one who bears witness that there is no god but Allah dying under bombardment in the lands of the Muslims. And if it narrows you return home and find the worry of raising children in the West, and what our children meet in the schools, and campaigns waged against Muslims or speaking in their name. And whoever is in this state, his anxiety is natural; we are human.

Yet in the Book of Allah there is a sentence that some people take as a sedative, and which for those who understand is a point of departure for strength: ﴿وَمَن يَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى اللَّهِ فَهُوَ حَسْبُهُ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ بَالِغُ أَمْرِهِ ۚ قَدْ جَعَلَ اللَّهُ لِكُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدْرًا﴾ [الطلاق: 3] (And whoever relies upon Allah, then He is sufficient for him. Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose. Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent.) Three sentences that move the whole system of thinking from the side of anxiety to the side of affirmation.

### Three sentences that sum up the road

"Then He is sufficient for him": Allah acts on your behalf, arranging the hidden and the apparent of affairs, dominant over the situation. "Indeed, Allah will accomplish His purpose": so no command is issued except by His command, and no one acts in His dominion except by His leave, so there is no power for the weak over the strong, nor for the poor over the rich, nor for the one of little resource over the one in command. "Allah has already set for everything a [decreed] extent": so the appearance of a phenomenon, or a person, or a word, or a program, or a persecution, all of that is decreed with the Lord of Might.

And the place of the verse in Sūrat al-Ṭalāq deserves a pause. The crises that pass over men mostly deal with their minds and bodies; and we were created as men to contend with this world, to challenge challenges, to be patient with problems and to rise after every fall, not to weep and not to withdraw. And in this sūra feelings are mixed with patterns of thinking: my relationship with work is a relationship of numbers, but my relationship with my wife is a "solemn covenant,"[^1] so when separation occurs it is as if the Lord of the worlds is telling me that reliance on Him is in the hardest and most difficult of times, even for men.

### A reliance that does not resemble sitting back

If you trace the contexts of reliance in the Muṣḥaf you find its expressions very many, and it alone occurs, according to what the preacher mentioned, more than seventy times in different forms. So let us begin from the first.

Nūḥ, peace be upon him, says to his people: ﴿يَا قَوْمِ إِن كَانَ كَبُرَ عَلَيْكُم مَّقَامِي وَتَذْكِيرِي بِآيَاتِ اللَّهِ فَعَلَى اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ﴾ [يونس: 71] (O my people, if my residence and my reminding of the signs of Allah has become burdensome upon you - then I have relied upon Allah). Is it conceivable that this was his only word? His reliance was not a withdrawal from the people, but an effort continued over nine hundred and fifty years.[^2] As if he took his heart out of his chest and deposited it in Allah's hand, and nothing remained in his hands but a body and a mind that work: when the heart speaks it says "O Lord," and when the hands speak they say "the means."

And Hūd, peace be upon him, says the same sentence and adds another dimension: ﴿إِنِّي تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ رَبِّي وَرَبِّكُم ۚ مَّا مِن دَابَّةٍ إِلَّا هُوَ آخِذٌ بِنَاصِيَتِهَا ۚ إِنَّ رَبِّي عَلَىٰ صِرَاطٍ مُّسْتَقِيمٍ﴾ [هود: 56] (Indeed, I have relied upon Allah, my Lord and your Lord. There is no creature but that He holds its forelock. Indeed, my Lord is on a path [that is] straight.) What is the significance of "holds its forelock"? There is no face on the face of the earth, whether you love it or hate it, that appears on the screens and writes the tweets, but its forelock is in Allah's hand. He holds the forelock of the one who transgresses, and holds the forelock of the one who is patient and bears, of the one who is tyrannical and slanders, and of the one who takes seeking reward as a method. And everything that crawls, including me and you, its forelock is in His hand. Then consider the ending: ﴿إِنَّ رَبِّي عَلَىٰ صِرَاطٍ مُّسْتَقِيمٍ﴾ (Indeed, my Lord is on a path [that is] straight). We repeat every day at least seventeen times "Guide us to the straight path," and this verse says that the path is what Allah made a method; and whoever deviates from it does not know reliance.

### Shuʿayb: reliance, then supplication, then giving

And let no one suppose that if he says "we have relied on Allah" he has finished the matter and gone to sleep. This is Shuʿayb, peace be upon him, saying: ﴿قَدِ افْتَرَيْنَا عَلَى اللَّهِ كَذِبًا إِنْ عُدْنَا فِي مِلَّتِكُم بَعْدَ إِذْ نَجَّانَا اللَّهُ مِنْهَا ۚ وَمَا يَكُونُ لَنَا أَن نَّعُودَ فِيهَا إِلَّا أَن يَشَاءَ اللَّهُ رَبُّنَا ۚ وَسِعَ رَبُّنَا كُلَّ شَيْءٍ عِلْمًا ۚ عَلَى اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْنَا ۚ رَبَّنَا افْتَحْ بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَ قَوْمِنَا بِالْحَقِّ وَأَنتَ خَيْرُ الْفَاتِحِينَ﴾ [الأعراف: 89] (We would have invented against Allah a lie if we returned to your religion after Allah had saved us from it. And it is not for us to return to it except that Allah, our Lord, should will. Our Lord has encompassed all things in knowledge. Upon Allah we have relied. Our Lord, decide between us and our people in truth, and You are the best of those who give decision.)

Note that the sentence did not end at "upon Allah we have relied," but was followed by "Our Lord, decide between us." And this is the tongue of the caller who approaches people and enters their gatherings and offers them good. And reliance is not that I say "I have relied" and then go out into the world hoping from Allah for two customers or three. So what do we give? This is a question I repeat to myself and to people: what do we give for the sake of this religion? And in another place Shuʿayb says: ﴿إِنْ أُرِيدُ إِلَّا الْإِصْلَاحَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ﴾ [هود: 88] (I only intend reform as much as I am able). This is the tongue of the working, moving leader who believes in his cause and carries out the will of his Lord, who knows no term but "work," joined with reliance.

### Mūsā: to submit yourself, not merely to believe in a cause

And Mūsā, peace be upon him, puts us in an awkward position when he speaks of reliance: ﴿وَقَالَ مُوسَىٰ يَا قَوْمِ إِن كُنتُمْ آمَنتُم بِاللَّهِ فَعَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلُوا إِن كُنتُم مُّسْلِمِينَ﴾ [يونس: 84] (And Moses said, "O my people, if you have believed in Allah, then rely upon Him, if you should be Muslims."). How much fear, hiding from the eyes, and avoiding confrontations make us sit back! "We don't want a clash with anyone, we hide and eat our bread, we came to America to gather a couple of pennies and go back." This is what makes us sit back.

And see how he said "if you should be Muslims" and was not content with "believers." For the word islām in some of its places speaks the truth more strongly than the word īmān. Many believe in causes and do not engage with them, and there is a difference between one who believed in a cause and one who submitted himself to it. The condition of islām is that you submit yourself: that you have no choice in your own self, not in choosing the time, nor the place, nor the instrument, nor the act. The call to prayer comes to you and changes the pattern of your daily planning. And options are presented to you and you say: this is lawful, I accept it, and this is unlawful, I do not go near it, and this is a means of earning money that is rejected, and this has doubt in it so I abstain from it. That is islām.

### The messengers, Yaʿqūb and the two men: taking the means, then reliance

And on the tongue of the messengers to their peoples in Sūrat Ibrāhīm: ﴿وَمَا لَنَا أَلَّا نَتَوَكَّلَ عَلَى اللَّهِ وَقَدْ هَدَانَا سُبُلَنَا ۚ وَلَنَصْبِرَنَّ عَلَىٰ مَا آذَيْتُمُونَا ۚ وَعَلَى اللَّهِ فَلْيَتَوَكَّلِ الْمُتَوَكِّلُونَ﴾ [إبراهيم: 12] (And why should we not rely upon Allah while He has guided us to our [good] ways. And we will surely be patient against whatever harm you should cause us. And upon Allah let those who would rely [indeed] rely.).[^3] What we have done today is no more than gathering some Qur'anic sentences on one subject, and behold, before you is a road map: the true believer is the one who holds the reins of his heart, and moves in society with a strength he draws from Allah.

And Yaʿqūb, peace be upon him, was once powerless in every means, the matter was completely handed over and no resource remained to him, and yet he said to his sons: ﴿لَا تَدْخُلُوا مِن بَابٍ وَاحِدٍ وَادْخُلُوا مِنْ أَبْوَابٍ مُّتَفَرِّقَةٍ ۖ وَمَا أُغْنِي عَنكُم مِّنَ اللَّهِ مِن شَيْءٍ ۖ إِنِ الْحُكْمُ إِلَّا لِلَّهِ ۖ عَلَيْهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ ۖ وَعَلَيْهِ فَلْيَتَوَكَّلِ الْمُتَوَكِّلُونَ﴾ [يوسف: 67] (enter from one gate but enter from different gates; and I cannot avail you against [the decree of] Allah at all. The decision is only for Allah; upon Him I have relied, and upon Him let those who would rely [indeed] rely.) He took the means, then referred the matter to Allah.

And in the difficult situation the two men upon whom Allah had bestowed favor say: ﴿ادْخُلُوا عَلَيْهِمُ الْبَابَ فَإِذَا دَخَلْتُمُوهُ فَإِنَّكُمْ غَالِبُونَ ۚ وَعَلَى اللَّهِ فَتَوَكَّلُوا إِن كُنتُم مُّؤْمِنِينَ﴾ [المائدة: 23] (Enter upon them through the gate, for when you have entered it, you will be predominant. And upon Allah rely, if you should be believers.) "Enter," that is, do not sit in the houses. For in the hours of crises, and in times of turmoil and the loss of anchoring in realities, we have in our hands nothing but Allah. But the question remains: what do we give?

### Where are our names in society?

When will all our names have a respected place within this society? We should not think that the most we do is to stand at a ballot box. What is required is a real presence in the details of society, and a participation that multiplies time after time. And perhaps that is a path to more challenges, but it is the path of the prophets and messengers, and we will not attain Paradise this easily.

### The Ever-Living who does not die

And in the direct command to rely our Lord says: ﴿وَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَى الْحَيِّ الَّذِي لَا يَمُوتُ وَسَبِّحْ بِحَمْدِهِ ۚ وَكَفَىٰ بِهِ بِذُنُوبِ عِبَادِهِ خَبِيرًا﴾ [الفرقان: 58] (And rely upon the Ever-Living who does not die, and exalt [Allah] with His praise. And sufficient is He to be, with the sins of His servants, Acquainted.) The point of the indication: that all people die: a president and a vice-president and a minister of war and a minister of defense and a professor and an adviser and a governor, each reaches in his tyranny, slander, aggression and blows whatever he reaches and then dies, whereas I rely on the One who does not die.

And this verse is wonderful; you cannot connect its parts well except through faith: "and rely," then "and exalt with His praise," then "and sufficient is He, with the sins of His servants, Acquainted." And the relation of sins to reliance and following the means is that whoever is good at avoiding his sin turns to his obedience, and whoever turns to his obedience seeks the greatest ways to Paradise, and there is none greater than the path of calling, with all its difficulties.

And like it in Hūd: ﴿وَلِلَّهِ غَيْبُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ وَإِلَيْهِ يُرْجَعُ الْأَمْرُ كُلُّهُ فَاعْبُدْهُ وَتَوَكَّلْ عَلَيْهِ ۚ وَمَا رَبُّكَ بِغَافِلٍ عَمَّا تَعْمَلُونَ﴾ [هود: 123] (And to Allah belong the unseen [aspects] of the heavens and the earth and to Him will be returned the matter, all of it, so worship Him and rely upon Him. And your Lord is not unaware of that which you do.) Nothing happens in Allah's dominion except what He knows, and nothing is in His creation except what He wills, and we will not face anything except that it is part of our provision. So repent to Allah from the feeling of fear, repent to Allah from leaning on other than His power, repent to Allah from not following the means and engaging. Establish your presence in the place where you are.

### Do not belittle a means

Our Messenger ﷺ did not cease to take part with people in the details of their lives as long as he was a messenger, and events and assaults did not make him sit back. So establish your presence with whatever means you own. And let me give you a simple example at which people laugh: imagine someone saying: "By Allah, the team working with me became Muslim because of candy!" How? Every day I take some candy in my pocket and with it I bring in a smile and say a tasbīḥ, and people ask: what is this sweetness? Who are you? And I say: a Muslim, and with him character, and prayers on their times, and commitment to good manners and lowering the gaze and respect for people, and he bears witness that there is no god but Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah. I gave this example so that we learn from it not to belittle a means: establish your presence in yourself and Allah establishes it in the souls of those around you, and apply Islam in your character and Allah will affect by it those around you, and do not belittle a word that repeats on your tongue, for its echo is in the hearts of those around you.

And remember that the call of Islam at the start of its affair was a word the Prophet ﷺ spoke on top of the mountain: «Do you see, if I told you that cavalry behind this valley wanted to raid you, would you believe me?» They said: We have never tried a lie from you.[^4] Then he called them to Islam. So see what you have deposited of yourself in people's souls: have they never tried from you a lie, nor betrayal, nor crookedness, nor cheating nor forgery?

### What is in your hands

The means with which Allah has enabled us we will be asked about before the Bestowing King. So O Allah, use us with the means You have deposited in our lives, and show us from ourselves good before You. O Allah, do not entrust us to ourselves nor to the whisperings of our devils nor to the whims of our souls, and make us firm with the firm word in the life of this world and in the Hereafter, and protect the blood of the Muslims in every place on earth.
