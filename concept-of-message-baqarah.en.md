*A structural reading of Sūrat al-Baqara that uncovers the concept of the message through the patterns of its reception: the classification of souls, then the models of the recipients (Adam, the Children of Israel, and Ibrāhīm), then the system of obligations by which the believing society is built. By Dr. Ahmed Abouseif, President of the American Imams Academy.*

## Preface: The Qur'an as a Continuous Address to the Patterns of Its Recipients
Praise be to Allah, who sent down upon His servant the Book and made therein no crookedness, and prayers and peace be upon the one sent as a mercy to the worlds, and upon his family and companions and whoever is guided by his guidance until the Day of Judgment. To proceed: the noble Qur'an, from its opening to its close, from Sūrat al-Fātiḥa to Sūrat al-Nās, is a continuous address directed to the human being as a recipient of the call. In its entirety it is an eloquent treatment of the patterns of people in their reception of the message: the one who turns to it and submits, opening his heart to guidance; the one who turns away and denies, closing its doors against it; and the hesitant deceiver, who shows other than what he conceals. The stories in the Qur'an are nothing but a mirror in which the images of this reception are reflected across the succession of nations; and legislation in it is nothing but a firmly built structure for whoever accepted the message and so became fit to carry its trust.

Sūrat al-Baqara, the longest of the Qur'anic sūras and the most comprehensive of them in rulings, is a condensed model of this great meaning. In it are arranged, in an interpretive reading that we present as an analytical vision, not as a definitive division or a binding delimitation, three great building blocks that lead one into another: a framing of the patterns of recipients at its opening, then a presentation of the models of reception through the stories, then a practical legislative construction of the believing society. This structural progression is what we here call "the concept of the message" as it is manifested in this comprehensive sūra.

Our method in this reading is to make the structure of the sūra speak from within, following the sequence of its passages not in order to impose a prior mould upon it, but to listen to its own arrangement: how it begins by classifying hearts, then follows with the setting forth of examples from the histories of nations, then makes a third by sending down obligation upon those among them whose reception was sound. This thematic unity that arranges the long sūra in a single pattern is among the aspects of its inimitability to which the verifying exegetes have drawn attention, for its verses are not scattered islands but links in one connected chain, the beginning of which holds to its end.

## (1) The Introduction in the Sūra: The Three Patterns of People as a Framing of Reception
The sūra opens with the disjointed letters ﴿الٓمٓ﴾ (Alif, Lām, Mīm), announcing the challenge and inimitability, then affirms that this Book contains no doubt, a guidance for the God-fearing.[^1] Here begins the wonderful classification of the kinds of recipients, and people are divided, according to their reception, into three kinds:
The first kind: the God-fearing believers, whom He, glory be to Him, described in His words ﴿الَّذِينَ يُؤْمِنُونَ بِالْغَيْبِ وَيُقِيمُونَ الصَّلَاةَ وَمِمَّا رَزَقْنَاهُمْ يُنفِقُونَ﴾ (Who believe in the unseen, establish prayer, and spend out of what We have provided for them);[^2] these accepted the message with complete acceptance: belief in the heart, performance by the limbs, and giving of wealth, so that the horizon of reception was complete in them. Their description came in five firmly built verses that sum up the pillars of faith that acts and bears fruit.
The second kind: the disbelievers, ﴿إِنَّ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُوا سَوَاءٌ عَلَيْهِمْ أَأَنذَرْتَهُمْ أَمْ لَمْ تُنذِرْهُمْ لَا يُؤْمِنُونَ﴾ (Indeed, those who disbelieve, it is all the same for them whether you warn them or do not warn them, they will not believe);[^3] this is reception by total turning away, where the heart and hearing have been sealed and the sight covered; so warning and its absence are equal to them, and their description came in two concise verses that fit the closing of their door against guidance.

The third kind: the hypocrites, whom the sūra exposes at length in thirteen verses ﴿وَمِنَ النَّاسِ مَن يَقُولُ آمَنَّا بِاللَّهِ وَبِالْيَوْمِ الْآخِرِ وَمَا هُم بِمُؤْمِنِينَ﴾ (And of the people are some who say, "We believe in Allah and the Last Day," but they are not believers);[^4] this is the ambiguous reception that shows acceptance and conceals rejection, and so it deserved several times the exposition that others received, because its danger is more hidden and its disease deeper. In this framing there is a profound structural wisdom: that the reception of the message, in every time and place, hardly departs from these three patterns, so presenting them at the opening is like the key by which the rest of the sūra is understood.

## (2) The First Part: Models of Receiving the Message through the Qur'anic Stories
When we move from the summary framing to the narrative embodiment, we see the sūra presenting three living models that embody the patterns of reception on the stage of history: a foundational model, an obstinate model, and an ideal model.

### The Foundational Model: Adam, peace be upon him
The sūra began with the foundational model, presenting the scene of vicegerency ﴿وَإِذْ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلْمَلَائِكَةِ إِنِّي جَاعِلٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ خَلِيفَةً﴾ (And [mention, O Muhammad], when your Lord said to the angels, "Indeed, I will make upon the earth a successive authority"),[^5] then honored Adam with the noblest instrument by which revelation is received, which is knowledge ﴿وَعَلَّمَ آدَمَ الْأَسْمَاءَ كُلَّهَا﴾ (And He taught Adam the names, all of them);[^6] so his receiving of the trust and the obligation, in a distinguished setting in the presence of the prostrating angels and the arrogant Iblīs, was a declaration that this creature is prepared to carry the message through the capacity for knowledge deposited in him.

Then came the slip ﴿فَأَزَلَّهُمَا الشَّيْطَانُ عَنْهَا﴾ (But Satan caused them to slip out of it),[^7] followed by the return and repentance ﴿فَتَلَقَّى آدَمُ مِن رَّبِّهِ كَلِمَاتٍ فَتَابَ عَلَيْهِ إِنَّهُ هُوَ التَّوَّابُ الرَّحِيمُ﴾ (Then Adam received from his Lord [some] words, and He accepted his repentance. Indeed, it is He who is the Accepting of repentance, the Merciful).[^8] The lesson drawn: the completeness of the human being's receptivity to the instruments of knowledge, wisdom and faith, with the possibility of error remaining, then of turning back; the human being is not an angel of infallible nature, nor a devil stamped with evil, but is one who can rise through knowledge and repentance.

And here is a necessary doctrinal rule by which the slip of Adam, peace be upon him, is presented with the etiquette due to prophethood: it is a slip that was followed by selection, guidance and accepted repentance, as He, glory be to Him, said in another place ﴿ثُمَّ اجْتَبَاهُ رَبُّهُ فَتَابَ عَلَيْهِ وَهَدَى﴾ (Then his Lord chose him and turned to him in forgiveness and guided [him]);[^9] so this slip does not lower the station of prophethood, nor is it elaborated in a way that offends the etiquette due to the prophets, nor is it made light of until the obligation is thought easy. The middle view is that it was a human lapse that preceded the selection for the message, after which came purification and choosing, mentioned with a balance containing neither belittling nor exaggeration.

### The Model of Obstinate Reception: The Children of Israel
Then the Qur'anic breath lengthens in presenting the opposing model: the Children of Israel, whose address occupied a wide space of the sūra; their call was opened with a reminder of the favor ﴿يَا بَنِي إِسْرَائِيلَ اذْكُرُوا نِعْمَتِيَ الَّتِي أَنْعَمْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ﴾ (O Children of Israel, remember My favor which I have bestowed upon you),[^10] and the favor was met with obstinacy, not thanks. Among the forms of their obstinacy: their demand to see Allah with their own eyes ﴿لَن نُّؤْمِنَ لَكَ حَتَّى نَرَى اللَّهَ جَهْرَةً﴾ (We will never believe you until we see Allah outright),[^11] and their wrangling over the matter of the cow until it was made harder for them after the least of cows would have sufficed them ﴿إِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْمُرُكُمْ أَن تَذْبَحُوا بَقَرَةً﴾ (Indeed, Allah commands you to slaughter a cow).[^12]

Among them also: their breaking of the solemn covenants ﴿وَإِذْ أَخَذْنَا مِيثَاقَكُمْ لَا تَسْفِكُونَ دِمَاءَكُمْ﴾ (And [recall] when We took your covenant, [saying], "Do not shed each other's blood or evict one another from your"),[^13] their killing of the prophets without right ﴿وَيَقْتُلُونَ النَّبِيِّينَ بِغَيْرِ الْحَقِّ﴾ (and killing the prophets without right),[^14] and their distortion of the Word after having understood it ﴿يُحَرِّفُونَهُ مِن بَعْدِ مَا عَقَلُوهُ﴾ (distort it after they had understood it).[^15] This model is presented from the Qur'anic text itself, without an unjust generalization to every individual among them; for the Qur'an itself was fair and affirmed the exception ﴿لَيْسُوا سَوَاءً﴾ (They are not [all] the same),[^16] so what is blameworthy is the pattern of obstinate reception, not races and lineages; and they were called the worst of recipients only because knowledge reached them and they denied, and covenants were taken from them and they broke them, so knowledge and stubbornness were combined in their case.

The wisdom in lengthening the address to them in this Madinan sūra is evident; the nascent believing community in Madīna was neighbor to this model and mixed with it, so the Qur'anic presentation came as a warning to it not to follow the way of those who preceded it in receiving the Book: to take from the religion what suits its desires and leave what weighs on it, or to believe in some and disbelieve in some. The aim is educational before it is informative, a mirror presented to every generation of bearers of the message so that it may guard against the pitfalls of those before it.

### The Ideal Model: Ibrāhīm, peace be upon him
Against obstinacy rises the ideal model: Ibrāhīm the Intimate Friend, peace be upon him, who received the message with complete compliance and fulfilment ﴿وَإِذِ ابْتَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ رَبُّهُ بِكَلِمَاتٍ فَأَتَمَّهُنَّ قَالَ إِنِّي جَاعِلُكَ لِلنَّاسِ إِمَامًا﴾ (And [mention, O Muhammad], when Abraham was tried by his Lord with commands and he fulfilled them. [Allah] said, "Indeed, I will make you a leader for the people");[^17] so leadership here is the fruit of fulfilment, and the reward of complete reception is that the recipient becomes a model from whom others receive. Then the compliance of the heart was followed by a tangible practical construction ﴿وَإِذْ يَرْفَعُ إِبْرَاهِيمُ الْقَوَاعِدَ مِنَ الْبَيْتِ وَإِسْمَاعِيلُ﴾ (And [mention] when Abraham was raising the foundations of the House and [with him] Ishmael),[^18] accompanied by humble supplication ﴿رَبَّنَا تَقَبَّلْ مِنَّا﴾ (Our Lord, accept [this] from us); so in his model were completed: receiving, compliance, building and supplication.

And "the House" in its context is an eloquent symbol of the civilizational construction of faith, standing on pillars that do not collapse: a foundation of tawḥīd, bricks of action, and a goal of Allah's good pleasure. Then Ibrāhīm's vision extends to the future and he prays for his descendants that a messenger be sent ﴿رَبَّنَا وَابْعَثْ فِيهِمْ رَسُولًا مِّنْهُمْ﴾ (Our Lord, and send among them a messenger from themselves);[^19] so our Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was the fulfilment of his prayer, and the chain of the message was joined from the foundational model to the ideal model to the Seal of the Prophets. By this it becomes clear that the stories in al-Baqara are not a historical digression but a purposeful presentation of the patterns of receiving the message: foundational receptivity in Adam, obstinate stubbornness in the Children of Israel, and building compliance in Ibrāhīm.

## (3) The Second Part: The System of Practical Obligations for Building the Believing Society
After the sūra had settled the question "who receives the message?", it moved to the second question: "how does the society live by it?" So the system of practical obligations came as a firmly built, graduated construction, not scattered fragments of rulings. Its most prominent landmark is the change of the qibla ﴿فَوَلِّ وَجْهَكَ شَطْرَ الْمَسْجِدِ الْحَرَامِ﴾ (So turn your face toward al-Masjid al-Haram),[^20] a declaration of the independence of the believing community and the distinctness of its character, and of its unity being established on one center.

Then the obligations followed one after another building this society pillar by pillar: fasting as training of the will and purification of the self ﴿كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الصِّيَامُ كَمَا كُتِبَ عَلَى الَّذِينَ مِن قَبْلِكُمْ﴾ (decreed upon you is fasting as it was decreed upon those before you),[^21] retaliation to preserve lives and deter aggression ﴿كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الْقِصَاصُ فِي الْقَتْلَى﴾ (Prescribed for you is legal retribution for those murdered),[^22] Ḥajj to unify the direction and gather the word ﴿وَأَتِمُّوا الْحَجَّ وَالْعُمْرَةَ لِلَّهِ﴾ (And complete the Hajj and 'umrah for Allah),[^23] and the rulings of marriage and divorce to safeguard the family which is the building block of society ﴿الطَّلَاقُ مَرَّتَانِ﴾ (Divorce is twice).[^24]

Then comes the economic and moral construction: the prohibition of usury to cleanse wealth of injustice ﴿وَأَحَلَّ اللَّهُ الْبَيْعَ وَحَرَّمَ الرِّبَا﴾ (But Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden interest),[^25] the verse of lending, the longest verse in the Qur'an, to preserve rights and document transactions ﴿إِذَا تَدَايَنتُم بِدَيْنٍ إِلَى أَجَلٍ مُّسَمًّى فَاكْتُبُوهُ﴾ (when you contract a debt for a specified term, write it down),[^26] spending as mutual support and purification of wealth ﴿مَّثَلُ الَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ أَمْوَالَهُمْ فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ﴾ (The example of those who spend their wealth in the cause of Allah),[^27] and fighting, though the self dislikes it, to repel aggression and protect the call ﴿كُتِبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الْقِتَالُ وَهُوَ كُرْهٌ لَّكُمْ﴾ (Fighting has been enjoined upon you while it is hateful to you).[^28] These obligations are an integrated system: devotional, purifying the spirit; social, protecting the family; economic, cleansing wealth; and defensive, protecting the entity; all of it a practical construction of the community that accepted the message and so became fit to carry it.

## Conclusion: The Concept of the Message as a Single Structural Thread
By this it becomes clear that "the concept of the message" through Sūrat al-Baqara is arranged in one structural thread with connected links: a call that addresses the patterns of people at its opening (the God-fearing believer, the denying disbeliever, and the ambiguous hypocrite), then presents the models of reception through the stories (the foundational in Adam, the obstinate in the Children of Israel, and the ideal in Ibrāhīm), then builds the believing society through the system of practical obligations. In its entirety the sūra answers two questions: who receives the message? And how does he live by it?

And this, as has preceded, is an interpretive analytical reading that we present for reflection, not for imposition, and we do not claim in it to exhaust the meanings of the comprehensive sūra, for its meanings are wider than any view can encompass and richer than any spring can run dry for them. It is enough for us to hint at a structural unity that helps reflection and connects the opening of the sūra with its purposes, and success is from Allah alone, and He is the Guide to the straight path. And Allah knows best, and may Allah send blessings and peace upon our Prophet Muhammad and upon his family and companions, all of them.
