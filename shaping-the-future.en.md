Praise be to Allah. We praise Him, we seek His help, His guidance and His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah from the evils of our souls and from our bad deeds. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner; His is the dominion and His is the praise, in His hand are the keys of the heavens and the earth, and He is able to do all things. And I bear witness that our master and our Prophet Muhammad is His servant, His messenger, His chosen one among His creation and His beloved; he conveyed the message, fulfilled the trust, gave sincere counsel to the nation, and through him Allah lifted the gloom, so may Allah reward him on our behalf and on behalf of the nation of Islam with the best of rewards. To proceed:

The future is a word that many people do not think about, and our chief preoccupation is what we live today and what is in our hands. And if some of us do think about the future, our thinking is confined to children and money: what shall I save for tomorrow, what shall I build in such-and-such place, what shall I leave for my children. So come, let us see a snapshot from the life of the Prophet ﷺ to know how his Companions shaped their future.

### The pampered youth of Quraysh

One day the Messenger of Allah ﷺ is sitting among his Companions, when one of the young Companions passes by them, named Muṣʿab ibn ʿUmayr, wearing a coarse, patched garment. The eyes of the Prophet ﷺ overflow with compassion for this youth who was once called the pampered youth of Quraysh. He was so pampered that he could not walk barefoot, and people would smell his scent from afar and know that the one walking on the road was so-and-so, Muṣʿab ibn ʿUmayr, may Allah be pleased with him. And many reports express the extent of the material comfort he lived in.

Then one day he hears that the Prophet ﷺ is sitting among his Companions in a small house on the outskirts of Mecca called the house of al-Arqam ibn Abī al-Arqam, so he goes to him, listens to him and believes at his hands. But before him is a great obstacle: his mother, who will be furiously angry if she learns that he has become Muslim with Muhammad ibn ʿAbd Allāh ﷺ. So he hid his Islam from her and pretended to live his ordinary life, but one of the polytheists saw him praying and told her. So she withheld from him all the good she had. And this is the first weapon with which we pressure someone who has gone out of our control: to strip him of the comfort he is in. And it is a weapon used even with the holders of chairs everywhere, so they listen to what is said out of fear that the chair will be pulled from under them. But Muṣʿab, may Allah be pleased with him, refused her, and submitted to what the Messenger of Allah ﷺ wanted for him.

And another stage of prevention begins, as she orders him imprisoned, so he becomes a prisoner after having been the only free one who did as he pleased, whose authority over his parents' wealth was complete. Now he is in a small room into which a little food and water is brought to him, forbidden everything he used to enjoy of perfumes and bedding, when the girls of Quraysh used to vie around him. Then news reaches him of the emigration of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ to Abyssinia, and he wants to get out of this captivity and contrives ways until he gets out with them. And on the road, as it is related, his feet were seen dripping blood from walking on the sand, so someone gave him a pair of leather socks so that he could continue his journey. His feet were soft, unaccustomed to hardship, but he goes on his way until he completes his emigration to Abyssinia. Then he returns with those who returned, then emigrates to Madīna by the direct command of the Prophet ﷺ, who ordered him to set out with the noble delegation to Yathrib to teach them Islam.

And the missionary positions that Muṣʿab made in Madīna are of great importance; we learn from them to this day the arts of oratory and calling to Allah: how you win over your opponent, how you make a dialogue out of nothing, how you win over the one in front of you, how you convince with argument and proof. It is a complete school called the school of Muṣʿab ibn ʿUmayr in calling to Allah. The Prophet ﷺ chose him as an envoy to the people of Madīna on behalf of Islam as a whole, so he went and Allah opened at his hands the hearts of these people, so they entered Islam, and they are recording points in Muṣʿab's record of deeds for the day of meeting Allah.

### «How will you be when one of you goes out in the morning in one robe and in the evening in another?»

Then one day he passes in front of the Prophet ﷺ in such patched attire, and the Prophet ﷺ weeps at his condition. And perhaps the Companions noticed his weeping and wondered, so he ﷺ spoke first, as it is related, saying: «How will you be when one of you goes out in the morning in one robe and in the evening in another, and a dish is set before him and another is lifted, and you cover your houses as the Kaʿba is covered?»[^1] That is: what if the world were opened up to us until you saw one of you come to the dawn prayer in an elegant shirt and come to the night prayer in other clothes, and you sit at a table with varieties and dishes, and there are curtains in your homes?

The Companions said: O Messenger of Allah, we on that day will be better than we are today. Their logic was that we would then be free for worship. So he ﷺ said: «By Allah, you today are better than you will be on that day.» And here we pause at these words to grasp the secret of what the Messenger of Allah ﷺ related as he compared the two states of Muṣʿab. For the Prophet ﷺ said of him, as the preacher relates: I have not seen in Mecca anyone with finer hair, nor softer garment, nor more comfort than Muṣʿab ibn ʿUmayr. That honor in which he was before Islam, while enjoying worldly comfort, and when Islam came, nothing satisfied him with this except that he left everything for Allah.

### «And do not throw yourselves with your own hands into destruction»

Then come, let us watch the Companions as they deal with the means of this world and the Hereafter, and how shaping the future was with them. Abū Ayyūb al-Anṣārī, may Allah be pleased with him, stands, nearly eighty years old, at the gates of Constantinople. The fortresses are strong, no one can open them, and a soldier bursts onto the scene and throws himself into the ranks of the enemy until Allah grants the conquest at his hands. So the people say: this man risked himself, and Allah said: ﴿وَلَا تُلْقُوا بِأَيْدِيكُمْ إِلَى التَّهْلُكَةِ﴾ (and do not throw [yourselves] with your [own] hands into destruction). A correct logic on the surface! So Abū Ayyūb said: Enough, stop. We are the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ, and this verse was revealed about us. We, the Anṣār, gathered one day talking among ourselves that we had done what was upon us and had given with the Prophet ﷺ and sacrificed until Allah gave Islam victory and established it on the earth, so we consulted about sitting in our homes and looking after our wealth, our affairs and our families, so Allah revealed about us: ﴿وَأَنفِقُوا فِي سَبِيلِ اللَّهِ وَلَا تُلْقُوا بِأَيْدِيكُمْ إِلَى التَّهْلُكَةِ ۛ وَأَحْسِنُوا ۛ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ الْمُحْسِنِينَ﴾ [البقرة: 195] (And spend in the cause of Allah and do not throw [yourselves] with your [own] hands into destruction [by refraining]. And do good; indeed, Allah loves the doers of good.)[^2]

The verse was revealed contrary to what people think. It is as if one of us says: I have given and done, they ask me to build the mosque when I have given what I have given, so don't talk to me again, I have done what is upon me. Those people went out with their selves, their wealth, their blood and everything they owned, as emigrants, fighters and spenders, and they gave what was upon them and more, and when they said: enough, let us rest a little, there was revealed about them: do not throw yourselves with your own hands into destruction. So where is the destruction? The destruction is that you sit back from work, and stop extending your hand, your mind, your body and your effort for Allah, then you destroy yourself. But to set out and work and extend your hand with giving and be with the participants in the work, then you give life to your self, your heart, your body and your mind. And Abū Ayyūb cited it because it was revealed when they wanted to sit back, not when they wanted to go out. So every setting out in the right and every striving in the way of Allah is a giving of life to this self.

### The seventh of seven

And among the simple scenes that show us what the Companions sacrificed is what Khālid ibn ʿUmayr related, saying: ʿUtba ibn Ghazwān, may Allah be pleased with him, one of the early ones to Islam, addressed us and said: "To proceed: this world has announced its parting, and has turned away swiftly, and nothing of it remains but a dreg like the dreg in a vessel that its owner pours out." Then he said: "I have seen myself the seventh of seven with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, we had no food except the leaves of trees until our jaws were sore, so I picked up a cloak and tore it between me and Saʿd ibn Mālik, so I wrapped myself with half of it and Saʿd wrapped himself with half of it, and today none of us has become anything but a governor over one of the cities."[^3]

Seven individuals around the Prophet ﷺ find nothing but tree leaves, until their throats dried and their jaws became sore, and they have not a sip of water to moisten them. So what made them patient? A religion that fills hearts. They preferred to feed their hearts the sweetness of faith and the purification of the Qur'an, even if their bellies went hungry. And this is ʿUtba preaching while governor of all of Basra, but he reminds people of what the early ones gave in ransom for this religion.

How did they shape this future? They shaped it when they sacrificed for it. They shaped it when they took part in political, social and economic life. They shaped it when they proved to the whole world that Islam is worthy of making men. They shaped it when they knew that they had an impact in life, that they were leaving a mark in it, that they were teaching something of good in it. They shaped it when they knew the value of themselves through «lā ilāha illā Allāh, Muḥammadun rasūl Allāh.» They shaped it when each of them felt that he teaches his child character as he teaches him a sūra of the Qur'an, and that behind him is righteous offspring carrying «lā ilāha illā Allāh» on its shoulder and setting out with it across the length and breadth of life. They shaped it when they felt that Paradise is near and that they take pleasure in its presence as they breathe the air around them. They shaped the future because they wanted to shape it.

And as for those who sit back, their affair is otherwise. It is enough for them to have what fills their bellies three times a day, or more or less. It is enough for them to have kinds of coffee whose sweetness and bitterness they contemplate, and to pick brands in clothes and other things, and to show their craft in it. This is their only concern. And I do not forbid Allah's blessing to anyone, but combining the two paths is something only the one who balances this world and the Hereafter is able to do. And that is the work of the people of taqwā.

I say this, and I seek Allah's forgiveness for myself and for you.

### The second sermon: Saʿīd ibn ʿĀmir over Ḥimṣ

Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds, and the outcome belongs to the righteous, and there is no hostility except against the wrongdoers. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, the Sovereign, the True, the Manifest, and I bear witness that our master and our Prophet Muhammad is His servant and messenger ﷺ. To proceed:

A final report about those in authority: Saʿīd ibn ʿĀmir, whom ʿUmar ibn al-Khaṭṭāb, may Allah be pleased with him, appointed one day over Ḥimṣ, and what do you know of the land of al-Shām! Luxury. But the people of Ḥimṣ complain about Saʿīd to the caliph. They said: we have four matters in which we complain of him. As for the first, he does not come out to us until after the forenoon. As for the second, he does not answer anyone at night. As for the third, he stays away from coming out to us one day a month. And as for the fourth, a fainting fit overtakes him every now and then.

And ʿUmar had chosen for Ḥimṣ one of the most trusted of people with him, so he sat with him and with them while praying to Allah that his opinion of him not be disappointed. So he asked them and they said that. Then he asked Saʿīd for his answer, and he said: By Allah, I did not love to show people what is between me and Allah, but, O Commander of the Faithful, I have no servant, so I am delayed until I prepare the food of my family and my household before I go out to them. As for the second, I have given them the day and given the night to Allah, so why do they want to share with me my portion with my Lord? As for the third, I have nothing but this garment, I wash it and wait for it until it dries once a month. And as for the fourth, I was with the disbelievers when they killed Khubayb ibn ʿAdī, and they were cutting pieces from his body and saying to him: would you like Muhammad to be in your place? And he would say: By Allah, I would not like the Prophet ﷺ to be pricked by a thorn while I am safe in my self, my family and my wealth.[^4]

Thus was the sacrifice of these people, and those were the features of their lives. They wanted the Hereafter and strove for it with its due striving and sought its way.

### Two questions for each one of us

Servants of Allah, we must stop with ourselves and ask two questions. The first question: do we truly want to shape the future, or are we content with what others shape for us? Do we truly want to contribute with our names, our works and our effort to what we will leave to the coming generations, or do we leave them to those who innovate in the world of devices and phones what captures the minds of children, and promote in it whatever they like?

And the second question: are you ready to help, prepared to sacrifice time, effort or money? That is an answer that no one answers except each for himself, and I cannot answer for anyone, for what is between you and Allah no one knows but Allah.

O Allah, make up for our shortcoming in the right of Your religion and the call to it, O Lord of the worlds. O Allah, use us and do not replace us.
