> Source: A transcription of a video sermon by Dr. Ahmed Abouseif — [Watch the original video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7XFzQNf0D8)

All praise is due to Allah; we praise Him, seek His help, seek His guidance and seek His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah from the evils of our own selves and from our misdeeds. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and I bear witness that our master and Prophet Muhammad is His servant and Messenger ﷺ. To proceed:

Congratulations on this blessed edifice; I ask Allah to place it in your scales. Whenever I come to this mosque I see in the eyes of its youth the future of Islam.

It would not befit a speaker in these days to pass over the Hijra of the Prophet ﷺ, for it was a defining moment in the life of Islam as a whole: a state before it and a state after it. Yet it was only a stage in the work for this religion in the life of the Prophet ﷺ. Let me say it again: a stage in the work, not its end and not a rest from it. Is there anyone among you who wants to walk the very plan on which the Messenger of Allah ﷺ moved?

### A life that knows no day of rest

In all his life the Prophet ﷺ had not a single day purely for rest, his life was never free of a problem, and he never slept a night without carrying the concern of Islam and the call. Not a moment passed over him without his thinking how to fulfil this great message.

So whoever wants to walk in his footsteps should declare to his Lord from the very beginning that he is for Allah, that neither he himself nor his family has a share in him. His family and relatives are among those he calls: he strives to keep them firm upon the truth and to guide them, and takes them by the hand to the shore of salvation. Whoever has this as his concern does not quarrel with his wife over whether a cup is clean or dirty, does not occupy his mind with a spider on the wall, and is not held up by the petty matters of the common people who have no purpose and no goal.

And I remember the days of boyhood, when we longed to leave the table of the book for play in the street, and my father would say to me: You are a man with a message; you must not turn away from your book. Your future is not like theirs; if you share their play today, you will share their loss of purpose tomorrow. So stand firm, may Allah make you firm. Then the days turned and the years passed, and we saw ourselves taking up positions and taking the creatures by their forelocks to guidance, by the permission of the Lord of the worlds, while others had no concern except what is shown on television screens.

And some of our friends ask me: When do you sleep? And my answer, though there is a joke in it: In the grave. No one will come there to knock on the door and wake me, and we shall get a very long time of sleep before that. We are not here to sleep. We are not here to sleep. We are not here to sleep. Let alone to differ over trivialities and quarrel over matters that have no root and no consequence.

### As the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ did in Dār al-Arqam

So bring your ranks together, and be like the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ who gathered around him in Dār al-Arqam. What was their state? They would slip in until they reached the place where guidance was heard, and their hearts listened to the voice of prophethood. They had no concern by night or day except Islam, thought of nothing but the call, and took precautions only for the safety of the one who conveyed this call ﷺ.

Then time passes. I visited this place years ago, and today I see new faces and children who have become young men, ma shāʾ Allāh. The days turn with us, time runs out, health passes, and the trace remains. So what have you put forward for yourself before Allah? And the question of that day will not be only about the effort you exerted, but about the blessing you were given. Whoever spoke well, his Lord will take him to account: Why did you not give thanks for the talent? And whoever was a man of wealth and was asked to spend, then withheld, his Lord will ask him why he did not put forward what I gave you, for what you have is not from you, and our Lord has told us that He made us successors over the wealth in our hands.

### The Hijra was not the first solution

And the Prophet's ﷺ emigration from Mecca to Medina was not the first solution. Someone might say: These people do not listen to us, so let us simply go somewhere else. But he ﷺ did not do so until it was the last of the solutions, and it was not to save himself, his body or his comfort, but to preserve the religion.

And the first thing asked of us in order to carry out the task as the Prophet and his Companions carried it out is: to feel the responsibility. Do you truly feel that you are responsible for Islam? It is a hard question, by Allah. For among us are those who do not feel responsible even for their own homes, so his wife weeps and the children are hungry and he does not care, spending his money on what is forbidden or on what he desires. And we need men who are true to themselves. So what does the word "man" mean? Manhood is not that I adorn myself at night, but that I work by day and by night, give what I can, and tire and am exhausted. For the sake of what? For the sake of what remains and endures, or what passes and ceases? What lasts for us, servants of Allah, is the Hereafter, Paradise and the meeting with the Lord of the worlds. Not the phone screens on which we spend long, wasted time, nor the television and the films and series we sit before. What remains is a page of the Muṣḥaf, which is not recited merely for the sake of spending time or for the blessing of the hours, but so that we learn from it how to act upon it and apply its rulings in the soil of our lives.

And after feeling responsible for this religion, that we take care to have children who gather in the house of Allah, learning His word and taking from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ his counsel.

And one of the most important traits of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ was this feeling of responsibility, and the Prophet himself ﷺ reached a degree of concern for which his Lord addressed him: ﴿فَلَعَلَّكَ بَاخِعٌ نَّفْسَكَ عَلَىٰ آثَارِهِمْ إِن لَّمْ يُؤْمِنُوا بِهَٰذَا الْحَدِيثِ أَسَفًا﴾ [الكهف: 6] (Then perhaps you would kill yourself through grief over them, [O Muhammad], if they do not believe in this message, [and] out of sorrow). Have we read this verse as we recite Sūrat al-Kahf every week? Imagine the state of the Prophet ﷺ, whose concern, because the people did not respond, reached such a degree that he almost destroyed himself, so Jibrīl comes down and the Lord of the worlds reassures him and lightens it for him. As for one of us, he wakes up with no concern except the kinds of food on the table, when he will get to work, whom he will meet and with whom he will spend his time, and these are questions empty of substance because it is a life that is passing and a lifespan that is ending.

### Khabbāb and the answer of the Prophet ﷺ

The Prophet ﷺ used to train his Companions in this feeling and in giving for this religion. Khabbāb ibn al-Aratt, may Allah be pleased with him, came one day, while the disbelievers were torturing them severely, and the Prophet ﷺ was beside the Kaʿba, resting on his cloak. Khabbāb uncovered his back, with the marks of the whips, and said: O Messenger of Allah, do you not see what has reached us? Will you not supplicate Allah for us? What did Khabbāb ask for? Only a supplication. And the Prophet ﷺ, as the preacher related the report, became angry, and said: O Khabbāb, among those before you a man would be combed with iron combs from the parting of his head to the soles of his feet, and would be split in two halves, and that would not turn him back from his religion.[^1]

Perhaps we ask: Why did Khabbāb meet this severe tone? Because he was going to go off to his companions and relate to them the Prophet's answer, so that it would strengthen the resolve of the men and revive their determination after slackness or after thinking of a means of escape. And this is not among our principles: our Islam, our life and our call are one, and our goal is to give for Allah as long as we live, and we have nothing else in our hands to be occupied with.

So if you are of this kind, seek for yourself a place beside the Prophet ﷺ, on the right or on the left. And if you are one of them, know that you will shake hands with Abū Bakr and you will exchange stories with the Prophet, and you will tell your own story with the call. Perhaps you will sit in the gathering of ʿUthmān, may Allah be pleased with him, as he tells of his spending in the cause of Allah, and perhaps you will see Khālid ibn al-Walīd telling you of his feats with the sword, and you will tell him of your feats in calling people, even if it was through email and social media. What matters is how you use these tools.

### He expects no thanks from anyone

That is the goal of the believers, and those are the states of the trivial. And I do not think any of those sitting now is one of those. We are people of the call; we give what is in our hands for Allah and expect from people neither thanks nor anything else; rather the concern is this Islam, being saved by it, and reaching Allah. This is the goal of the people of intellect who grasp the realities and are not deceived by the world and what is in it.

I say this, and I seek Allah's forgiveness for myself and for you.
