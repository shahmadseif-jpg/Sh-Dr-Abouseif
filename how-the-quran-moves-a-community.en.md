> Occasion: A summary of a Friday sermon delivered at the Islamic Center of al-Isrāʾ on 30 March 2018
> Source: A transcription of a video sermon by Dr. Ahmed Abouseif — [Watch the original video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1rsrNfwZn58)

Why are we in the mosque? And with what do we leave it? And what do we do in our shops and our homes, in the times of our busyness and the times of our leisure? Where are we heading, and what will we come out of this world with?

Praise be to Allah. We praise Him, we seek His help, His guidance and His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah from the evils of our souls and from our bad deeds, and we bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and that Muhammad is His servant and messenger ﷺ. To proceed:

Let us speak for some minutes about the movement of societies through the noble Qur'an. And I set out from a question about the reality in which the nation and the societies around it live: can we describe what we see as a new Jāhiliyya, and that we need a treatment with which we begin anew?

### A Jāhiliyya harsher than the first

Compare the Jāhiliyya of Mecca when the Prophet ﷺ was sent in it with the Jāhiliyya people live today. You will find the Jāhiliyya of the people of Mecca modest next to what we see now. When Allah spoke of the display of the first Jāhiliyya,[^1] that display was modest beside this crude exposure that has broken into our lands and our homes; we ask Allah for well-being, and chastity for our sons, our daughters and our women. And compare the usury that the Jews practiced when the Prophet ﷺ was sent, and the harshly worded war the Qur'an waged against trading in it, with what we live today of forms that Satan has newly devised in the minds of many; you will find their usury very modest next to the usury around us.

And perhaps we need to reformulate life anew. And that is fine: the striving is from us and the success is from Allah, the movement is from us and the guidance is from Allah. We do not own people's hearts, but we own the effort toward them. We do not move the minds of creation, but we instill in them the ideas that lead them to Allah's guidance, and then to setting out on the right path. This is what we are able to do.

### The first revelation: knowledge

The first thing Allah revealed: ﴿اقْرَأْ بِاسْمِ رَبِّكَ الَّذِي خَلَقَ﴾ [العلق: 1] (Recite in the name of your Lord who created). And I ask: why this scene at the beginning? I see that societies collapse completely if the cognitive side is absent from the minds of people, and people live in chaos like cattle or worse. So if ignorance takes hold and the rites of religion are practiced night and day as mere worship, stripped of feelings and of actual movement, people live like cattle. This is why the first thing sent to the Prophet ﷺ was: be above the level of ignorance, above the level of illiteracy, and be a reader.

And what is the reading sought? I think, and Allah knows best, and our shaykh will correct us if we err, that it is not the reading of the line alone; for the Prophet ﷺ did not read the line, but he used to command his companions to write. It is the reading of the universe with everything in it. Whether it is the reading of a line or the reading of a universe, Allah commanded the Muslim society to be a learning society, so that it can rise to the summit; for without knowledge there is no summit, no excellence and no distinction. And if people become ignorant, walking in the caravan's train not knowing where to, or what to do, or what to hope for, their place is the garbage heap, may Allah honor you and keep you safe.

### The second revelation: character

When knowledge is set straight we need a second side. Many of the people of tafsīr say that the second thing revealed was Sūrat al-Qalam:[^2] ﴿وَإِنَّكَ لَعَلَىٰ خُلُقٍ عَظِيمٍ﴾ [القلم: 4] (And indeed, you are of a great moral character). A society that has knowledge but no character uses knowledge in vice, in spreading indecency, in corrupting minds and undermining the pillars of life. Knowledge without character is nothing.

Then Allah draws for us a picture that strikes our eyes like fantasy, so that we picture the society in this form: ﴿وَدُّوا لَوْ تُدْهِنُ فَيُدْهِنُونَ ۝ وَلَا تُطِعْ كُلَّ حَلَّافٍ مَّهِينٍ ۝ هَمَّازٍ مَّشَّاءٍ بِنَمِيمٍ ۝ مَّنَّاعٍ لِّلْخَيْرِ مُعْتَدٍ أَثِيمٍ﴾ [القلم: 9-12] (They wish that you would soften [in your position], so they would soften [toward you]. And do not obey every worthless habitual swearer, [and] scorner, going about with malicious gossip, a preventer of good, transgressing and sinful).[^3] These nine attributes are a picture of a loathsome society, all lying, deceit and showing off. In it a man is not safe regarding his wife, nor regarding himself in the hands of a doctor, nor does he trust a teacher's word to his pupil or to his son, nor goods he buys, nor information he acquires. And likewise people do not trust me. So we are afraid of one another all the time, we doubt one another at every stage. A terrifying society. But Allah opens the verse with His words: ﴿وَلَا تُطِعْ﴾ (and do not obey), that is, keep away from this kind of society, and make a society contrary to this picture. Truthfulness is our principle, clarity is our means, and reaching direct goals is our way.

### The third revelation: wealth, then the self

When the cognitive side and then the moral side are complete, and people look in Sūrat al-Qalam at the source of the trial of societies, which is wealth, Allah tells us about the owners of the garden when they conspired among themselves to go to their garden in the morning and pick its fruit, moving in secrecy like ants walking on the ground, not drawing people's attention. This is the seat of the disease, so beware of it and do not be deceived by it.

But there is another obstacle in the way of those who work for Allah, and it is what we have spent a great deal of time on while speaking of our suffering in dealing with those around us. The corruption of people's character weakens the resolve of those working for the religion, and makes me forever wavering between a step I take and another I hold back, between a word I say and another I conceal, between testimony I give and reserve I keep. For this reason the caller, who is each one of you, needed a source from which to receive confidence and fill his heart with certainty. So after that was revealed: ﴿يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُزَّمِّلُ ۝ قُمِ اللَّيْلَ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا ۝ نِّصْفَهُ أَوِ انقُصْ مِنْهُ قَلِيلًا ۝ أَوْ زِدْ عَلَيْهِ وَرَتِّلِ الْقُرْآنَ تَرْتِيلًا ۝ إِنَّا سَنُلْقِي عَلَيْكَ قَوْلًا ثَقِيلًا ۝ إِنَّ نَاشِئَةَ اللَّيْلِ هِيَ أَشَدُّ وَطْئًا وَأَقْوَمُ قِيلًا ۝ إِنَّ لَكَ فِي النَّهَارِ سَبْحًا طَوِيلًا﴾ [المزمل: 1-7] (O you who wraps himself [in clothing], arise [to pray] the night, except for a little, half of it - or subtract from it a little, or add to it - and recite the Qur'an with measured recitation. Indeed, We will cast upon you a heavy word. Indeed, the hours of the night are more effective for concurrence [of heart and tongue] and more suitable for words.) This is the place of receiving that supports the self and steadies it, so that the caller stands the stance of the confident when he speaks the truth, declaring it openly in the hearing and sight of people: «a heavy word» that you receive by night and then set out with by day.

### The fourth revelation: conveying

When these three come together, the cognitive side, the moral side, and the settled, tranquil psychological side, it is right for you to speak what you have and begin spreading your idea. Then the call comes: ﴿يَا أَيُّهَا الْمُدَّثِّرُ ۝ قُمْ فَأَنذِرْ﴾ [المدثر: 1-2] (O you who covers himself [with a garment], arise and warn). Now your knowledge is complete. Those who inherited the revelation from him ﷺ inherited with it the conveying. And the cognitive side was complete in him ﷺ, and his character was complete until his Lord bore witness to him, and his self was complete and at peace, and he knew that the way to the tranquility and calm of the heart is only prayer, so he used to say: «Give us rest through it, O Bilāl.»[^4] Then comes the turn of declaring the truth openly, and the role of the media in spreading the word of Islam.

And the media is an extremely dangerous means; it supports falsehood and backs it, or it crushes truth and undermines its pillars. And the Prophet ﷺ used it alongside the sword and the striking hand: a skilled tongue, and noble poets who surrounded him representing the media front defending Islam. And there is the internal media whose voice rises in the midst of people with «lā ilāha illā Allāh, ḥayya ʿalā al-ṣalāh, ḥayya ʿalā al-falāḥ» (there is no god but Allah, come to prayer, come to success), so they come in answer to the call and find the voice of truth spreading the teachings of Allah in their midst.

### The fifth revelation: Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds

When these four pillars are complete, the cognitive, moral, psychological and media, the Muslim, as an individual and as a society, is able to say: Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds. And here comes the fifth revelation: ﴿الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ ۝ مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ﴾ [الفاتحة: 2-4] ([All] praise is [due] to Allah, Lord of the worlds, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful, Sovereign of the Day of Recompense). And it was not a passing revelation, but settled into the root of legislation and worship, until the Muslim does not complete his prayer without reciting this slogan, raising it seventeen times a day at least. This feeling makes me speak with my tongue not about myself nor for myself alone, but I speak as a representative of the nation when I say: ﴿اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ﴾ [الفاتحة: 6] (Guide us to the straight path), and when I say: ﴿إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ﴾ [الفاتحة: 5] (It is You we worship and You we ask for help). I am not alone, but I am with my brothers and those around me.

### Conclusion: a Qur'an walking on the earth

This is what we wanted to lay out in reformulating the Muslim mindset: how we deal with the Qur'an, how we look at it, how we find in its sūras solutions to the problems of our daily lives, and how we make it a true beacon. And the greatest and briefest description of the Prophet's ﷺ state is that short phrase that came from the closest of people to him, his wife: his character was the Qur'an.[^5] He did not move except from a verse, did not sit except from a Qur'anic scene, and no verse passed him but he was either applying a command, or stopping at a prohibition, or seeking forgiveness in a state, or praying for a reward, or seeking refuge from a punishment. And the Sunnah is only a mirror on which the Qur'an was reflected when it came down.

So hold fast to what has been revealed to you, and hold tight to this method. And beware of thinking that when you have gathered a hundred thousand or fifty or become a millionaire you have accomplished something; it is only a little of what Qārūn had, and then the matter comes in less than a moment: ﴿فَخَسَفْنَا بِهِ وَبِدَارِهِ الْأَرْضَ فَمَا كَانَ لَهُ مِن فِئَةٍ يَنصُرُونَهُ مِن دُونِ اللَّهِ وَمَا كَانَ مِنَ الْمُنتَصِرِينَ﴾ [القصص: 81] (And We caused the earth to swallow him and his home. And there was for him no company to aid him other than Allah, nor was he of those who [could] defend themselves). I want to be among the victorious, and this is not the method. The method is to be encompassing of this Book and the sources of receiving from it and the verses and lessons in it. We ask Allah to take us by the hand to what holds our salvation in this world and the Hereafter, and to keep our steps safe and ease our affairs. And we seek refuge in You, O Allah, from worry and grief, from incapacity and laziness, from cowardice and miserliness, and from being overcome by debt and the domination of men.[^6] And praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
