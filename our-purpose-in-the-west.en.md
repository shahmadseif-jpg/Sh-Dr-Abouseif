Imagine the Prophet ﷺ sitting around the Kaʿba at the beginning of the call, reflecting on the future of this religion, with no concern in his life greater than it. Then imagine him today entering your mosque, seeing the colors of the people, their tongues and their backgrounds, from which lands they came, and how many dialects have gathered. Would he not rejoice at your presence, and take comfort that Allah has prepared for His religion on earth a people? Congratulations to whoever was a cause of making the Prophet ﷺ happy in the presence of his Lord. And on this scene the preacher built his question: What is our purpose in this land?

### Why are you here?

From the moment you decided to set out from your country to here, and then decided a graver decision, which is to have your children here: What is the concern? What is the purpose? What is the reason? The preacher says that daily dealings have taught us that those who came to America to gather great wealth ended up turning to the Lord of the worlds and leaving their wealth. And this land, as he says, knows how to give you and take from you again in the same moment, and leaves nothing to anyone, and everyone competes and runs after an illusion called gathering wealth, improving conditions and increasing income.

But this happens in every part of the earth, for the days pass over people, and Allah has granted them ease of living that they get used to after a while, or hardship of living that they adapt to after a while. Look at the people of Gaza, how they live and how the day passes over them and where they eat from. And move to Somalia or Mali or lands where there is hardship in livelihood, and you will see a smile on many faces; they weep at times and laugh at others. Then the preacher sums up: the summary of the world is death, the summary of life is a grave, and the summary of the days is standing before the Lord of the worlds.

### Allah knows best where to place His message

And the question remains: Why are we here? Whoever says "I am the one who came" has misjudged, for Allah is the One who gathers people in the land He wants for them, at the time He knows their presence in it is good. ﴿اللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ حَيْثُ يَجْعَلُ رِسَالَتَهُ ۗ سَيُصِيبُ الَّذِينَ أَجْرَمُوا صَغَارٌ عِندَ اللَّهِ وَعَذَابٌ شَدِيدٌ بِمَا كَانُوا يَمْكُرُونَ﴾ [الأنعام: 124] (Allah is most knowing of where He places His message. There will afflict those who committed crimes debasement before Allah and severe punishment for what they used to conspire). Reflect on this short text: Allah knows best which heart takes in the message, which mind reflects upon it, on which tongue it is entrusted, which body cares for it, and likewise which city and which state and which country, and which time.

### Carriers of the religion or adherents of it?

And here is a simple question: Are you among the carriers of this religion or among its adherents? There is a great difference between one who entered Islam and found it comforting to himself, reassuring to his heart, calming to his nerves, and saw in it a system that runs life; the dawn prayer and peace, and the night prayer and quiet sleep with a reassured heart, and the matter settled. And a person to whom is said what was said to the Prophet ﷺ in Sūrat al-Kahf: ﴿فَلَعَلَّكَ بَاخِعٌ نَّفْسَكَ عَلَىٰ آثَارِهِمْ إِن لَّمْ يُؤْمِنُوا بِهَٰذَا الْحَدِيثِ أَسَفًا﴾ [الكهف: 6] (Then perhaps you would kill yourself through grief over them, [O Muhammad], if they do not believe in this message, [and] out of sorrow). Will you kill yourself from the abundance of concern and bearing responsibility because they did not become Muslims? This is the greatest responsibility that was entrusted to the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and the prophets before him, for a covenant of conveying was taken from them, and they bore it as a trust and delivered it to the people at the same level of excellence at which the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was.

And we? We go out in the morning to our jobs, vexed by a long day, weighed down with many burdens, and we return to our homes at the end of the day with strength exhausted and determination gone, so a man returns with his worries to his home and sleeps till morning and then opens his eyes worried about a new day. And the link between my heart and mind and the heavens is missing in its greatest concern, which is the concern of conveying the religion.

The preacher gives an example: if I asked you about your job, you would say that you are a doctor or an engineer or a teacher, and what the job requires. But this job is number two. My first task in life is that I am charged by the Lord of the worlds with conveying the message, and that I am an heir to the inheritance of the Prophet ﷺ. So do we convey this understanding to the minds and hearts of people so that they rise to the matter of their call, and bear its responsibility in wealth, effort, time and giving? Or do we sit around the table of food that is called provisions, gathering around it and guarding it, and do not expose ourselves to danger lest we expose the table to danger? We fear for our children from every whisper and touch, and for our provision from every possibility.

### Abū Bakr: I left for them Allah and His Messenger

And this is contrary to the state of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ. The Messenger of Allah ﷺ would stand on his pulpit and ask: O people, we want money for such-and-such, so who is the first to raise his hand? Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq, the first to respond to the Messenger of Allah, not in money only, but in every call the Prophet ﷺ made. He would go to his house and then return with the money, and bring all his money. Even if his capital grew many times over, he would bring all of it. And his answer when the Messenger of Allah ﷺ asked him: What did you leave for your family? was: "I left for them Allah and His Messenger."[^1]

This though he was a skilled merchant, but the "business" mentality says: I must have a balance, a financial reserve, strategies and a stock of goods. As for Abū Bakr, he started from zero almost every time. Because he was certain that the sender of provisions is Allah, so he did not look at what was in his pocket as something that would cover him during the day or the week. The One who works in these means is the One who causes them, glory be to Him. So whoever has the skill of trade and has with it the strength of certainty in what is with the Lord of the worlds puts 100% of his money in the deal, certain that it will return to him.

### After a hundred years

Servants of Allah, we are here to convey a trust, to fulfil a message, and to carry this religion. We shall all die, after ten years, indeed after ten days, indeed perhaps after hours. And if we say: a hundred years from today, who among us thinks he will still be above the ground? No one. And after we are under the ground: either we shall have left behind us an Islam moving on the earth as the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ did, or we shall have gone to what is under the ground and relieved humanity of the burden we used to place on it.

Then, we are in a time that turns with its people, and the saved in it is the one who holds fast to his religion, as in the ḥadīth the preacher cited by its meaning: that a time will come upon people when the one who is patient upon his religion is like one holding a hot coal.[^2] Imagine that you hold a coal you cannot bear for seconds, it burns your hand, and yet you hold on to it. That is the matter of the religion: whoever holds fast to it is saved, and whoever neglects it is destroyed.

### A mosque that trains imams

And this is the very purpose that the preacher and his brothers decided to follow for many years, since they came to this country: they pledged to the Lord of the worlds that they would be among the carriers of this trust in this land. And he says that they now have in the Dallas area of Texas a mosque that is not only a place of prayer, but a place for training imams, teaching them how to ascend these pulpits, lead people in the prayers, resolve problems between spouses, and do justice to the wronged. "We train them on what we were trained on and more," and the only difference is the difference of language and culture, so they add to what they learned at al-Azhar what the need in this country dictates of newly arising conditions.

And he made clear that giving the students what they need, and keeping the doors of the mosque open, the lights shining and the carpets laid, happens only through the spending of righteous people like them, and he asked Allah to make them among those who race to good deeds.

He closed with supplication: O Allah, make us pleased with Your pleasure, make us firm with a firmness from You, make us reach what You want wherever we are, and guide us to the good things You want us for. O Allah, be for every oppressed person on earth, and be for our people in Palestine and Gaza.
