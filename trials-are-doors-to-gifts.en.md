> Source: A transcription of a video sermon by Dr. Ahmed Abouseif — [Watch the original video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SXdddMWz7G0)

Is trial something that befalls life from outside, or a part of its makeup? Let us look first at ourselves: none of us came out into the wide world except after passing through the narrowness of the womb, nor was able to eat except after hardship, nor to set out in life except after learning much. And no one walks strongly except one whose stumbles were many before that.

Praise be to Allah. We praise Him, we seek His help, His guidance and His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah from the evils of our souls and from our bad deeds. Whomever Allah guides, none can lead astray, and whomever He leads astray, none can guide. We bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and that Muhammad is His servant and messenger ﷺ. I advise you and myself to have taqwā of Allah and hope in what is with Him. To proceed:

You have known that the title of this sermon is that gifts come from trials. It is a truth across the history of Islam, and if we said across the history of humankind we would be truthful. And we came to this place in compliance with His words, the Exalted: ﴿يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا نُودِيَ لِلصَّلَاةِ مِن يَوْمِ الْجُمُعَةِ فَاسْعَوْا إِلَىٰ ذِكْرِ اللَّهِ وَذَرُوا الْبَيْعَ﴾ [الجمعة: 9] (O you who have believed, when [the adhan] is called for the prayer on the day of Jumu'ah [Friday], then proceed to the remembrance of Allah and leave trade), we left for its sake what is behind us.

### Trial is a way that passes over everyone

At the level of the nation, you see from the beginning of creation Adam, peace be upon him: Allah created him and settled him in His Paradise, in a place anyone would wish to be in the like of, speaking to the angels and they speaking to him, and his Lord teaching him the names, all of them. And in the midst of this luxury the landmarks of life turn over, and behold Iblīs comes out from where you do not know to enmity toward this human being. He was beside me yesterday and today he is opposite me. And life passes and you see two brothers from one womb, with one food, raised by one hand, then one of them turns into an enemy of his brother and kills him. And that is the way of life in its general course.

Then you stand before Nūḥ, peace be upon him: nine hundred and fifty years trying to convince his people, while they try to demolish his powers and shatter his faculties. And you move on to Ibrāhīm, peace be upon him, and the hostility of his people to him reached the point that they threw him into the fire. And you pass through the system of the prophets and messengers: Yūsuf, peace be upon him, and the woman who solicited him was the cause of his imprisonment. So trials pass over people without exception.

And Mūsā, peace be upon him, was raised in the house of the king, and one like him among the most special of the special, no one stands in front of him and no one rejects a request of his. Then in a moment: ﴿وَجَاءَ رَجُلٌ مِّنْ أَقْصَى الْمَدِينَةِ يَسْعَىٰ قَالَ يَا مُوسَىٰ إِنَّ الْمَلَأَ يَأْتَمِرُونَ بِكَ لِيَقْتُلُوكَ فَاخْرُجْ إِنِّي لَكَ مِنَ النَّاصِحِينَ﴾ [القصص: 20] (And a man came from the farthest end of the city, running. He said, "O Moses, indeed the eminent ones are conferring over you [intending] to kill you, so leave [the city]; indeed, I am to you of the sincere advisors."). ﴿فَخَرَجَ مِنْهَا خَائِفًا يَتَرَقَّبُ﴾ [القصص: 21] (So he left it, fearful and anticipating [apprehension]). The son of the palace yesterday goes out fearful, looking around him. Why does the world turn with this logic, and events change to this degree? Then ʿĪsā, peace be upon him, a caller to Allah reminding people of the Book of Mūsā that preceded him, and they plotted against him until they nearly crucified him, had Allah not raised him to Himself.

### The trials of the Prophet ﷺ and his Companions

And you go on with time and see the Prophet ﷺ from the very first moment. One of the disbelievers says to him: «May you perish for the rest of the day! Is it for this that you gathered us?»[^1] Then he does not come out of one crisis but he enters another, and does not cross a stage but challenges follow one after another. You look at him in the Valley of Abū Ṭālib among his people when they were besieged with a harsh siege until none of them found anything to eat. And glory be to the All-Creating, All-Knowing, who supplies people with strength and inspires them with patience and good seeking of reward.

And I recall the ḥadīth of Khabbāb, may Allah be pleased with him, when he came to the Prophet ﷺ while he was sitting around the Kaʿba, and said: O Messenger of Allah, do you not see what has reached us? Do you not see what is upon us? And he uncovered his back, which the people's whips had pained, and he had been tortured severely. He said: Will you not ask for help for us? Will you not pray to Allah?[^2] And here we realize that all that Khabbāb asked for was supplication; he did not ask that the Prophet ﷺ fight or take up a sword. And it is an easy matter if he ﷺ had given it to him at the time. But Khabbāb is a place of teaching, and not he alone, but the Ummah after him wants to learn this lesson: that when hardships descend, there must be action for them, not mere words. So the Prophet ﷺ gives Khabbāb an example: «There was a man among those before you who would be combed with iron combs... that would not turn him from his religion... but you are impatient.»

May Allah send blessings upon His Prophet and be pleased with his Companions, how much they suffered in establishing the right and affirming the word of Allah! And how much effort they gave until this Islam reached us, which we enjoy now, and even rebel against in many of our states, and are unable to perform in its simplest form.

### Life is hardship and toil

And are trials a part of life or something that befalls it? You grasp that through a slight understanding of His words, the Exalted: ﴿لَقَدْ خَلَقْنَا الْإِنسَانَ فِي كَبَدٍ﴾ [البلد: 4] (We have certainly created man into hardship). And kabad is continuous suffering with no break and no rest from it, you go from one matter to another. Then you ask: until when? And you see the condition of people: he wants to grow up, and when he grows up he wants to graduate, and when he graduates he wants to marry, then to work, then to have children, then for them to grow up, then to marry them off. Then he says: O Allah, until when do I carry this worry? And his Lord answers him: ﴿يَا أَيُّهَا الْإِنسَانُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدْحًا فَمُلَاقِيهِ﴾ [الانشقاق: 6] (O mankind, indeed you are laboring toward your Lord with [great] exertion and will meet it).

And look at the significance of the phrasing: had it been said "thumma mulāqīhi" (then you will meet it), "thumma" in the language indicates delay, so we would have a rest period. But Allah said: ﴿فَمُلَاقِيهِ﴾, and the "fa" indicates sequence and immediate succession. So you keep running until you fall, and in life in general there is no moment called a moment of rest. And for this reason Allah was gentle with the believers and made their place of rest His remembrance: ﴿أَلَا بِذِكْرِ اللَّهِ تَطْمَئِنُّ الْقُلُوبُ﴾ [الرعد: 28] (Unquestionably, by the remembrance of Allah hearts are assured).

### The goal of those who knew the reality of this world

So if you look around you and see these hardships that the Ummah is passing through, which I do not call calamities, for each stage of them is a crossing to the one after it. And I recall a stance of one of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ, if the report is sound, who said: O Messenger of Allah, we have reached the heavens in glory and praise, and we seek above that a station. He ﷺ said: Where to? He said: To Paradise, O Messenger of Allah.[^3] That is the goal of those who knew Allah and the reality of this world, and wanted to leave behind traces. They measured the distance and expanse of this world and found it equals zero, unless you leave behind you a tongue that mentions your Lord and develops the universe with more worship of the Creator of the heavens and the earth. Life equals zero unless you leave it with the fullest salvation and your name is recorded among the names of those saved to Paradise, not to any other destination.

We say much and repeat slogans and chant words, but few carry out, and fewer persist in carrying out. So be servants of Allah, for calamities have never cleared except from those strong through their Lord. And among the hardest times in the life of the Prophet ﷺ passed over him on the day of Badr, while he was among his people, and Abū Bakr, may Allah be pleased with him, comes to him: O Messenger of Allah, sit in the shelter and pray to Allah for us.[^4] So the work is divided into two works: people over there striking with their hands, and a tongue here fervent to the heavens with supplication and seeking help: O Lord, all are Your servants. So what work do you do?

### A question to the people of this country

People of New York, which has become the Kaʿba of money in the world, what are you doing here? What are we doing in America? What do we want from this country? What do we achieve in it of good or benefit? What do we leave behind for our children? How much wealth people have gathered and how much they have spent, and how many buildings we have obtained and how many we have built, then left them behind our backs. And the servant does not know whether he will draw a breath after this or not, nor whether he will spend the night or not, nor whether a morning will dawn upon him. So what kind of world is in these heads, and what is it that wanders over these minds? Before us is work, before us is work.

And it is transmitted of the Prophet ﷺ in his house, on his bed, a sentence that spread and became a slogan for those working in the way of Allah, not in this country only but in every land: «The time of sleep has passed, O Khadīja.»[^5] People are killed and slaughtered here and there, and struck from above and below, and the people in Palestine are as you see, and suffering for the people of Islam in every country, and no land escapes a hardship, apparent or hidden. So what do we do? By Allah, besides whom there is no god, we are responsible before Allah for every breath that leaves our mouths, for every word that issues from us, and for every moment that passes us. O Lord of salvation.

### Conclusion: a supplication

O Allah, bring us to our aim in this place, and grant us its attainment by Your leave and generosity. O Allah, victory for Your servants in Palestine, and steadfastness for Your servants on the earth, O Most Merciful of the merciful. O Allah, enrich us with Your lawful over Your unlawful, and with Your bounty over all besides You. O Allah, inspire us with our right guidance, ease our affair for us, and fulfil our needs. And praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
