Faith, in the balance of the Qur'an, is a single cause of happiness in this worldly life and of bliss in the Hereafter, and its equation is brief and admits no appeal: ﴿وَمَنْ أَعْرَضَ عَن ذِكْرِي فَإِنَّ لَهُ مَعِيشَةً ضَنكًا﴾ [طه: 124] (And whoever turns away from My remembrance - indeed, he will have a depressed life).

Is this our reality? The reality that individuals live, and the reality that the Ummah lives? Look around you. The level at which the Ummah lives is that it is not counted for anything, neither as an enemy nor as a friend, and no one cares about attacking us at the time he wishes, to the extent he wishes, and wherever he wishes; we are on the margins of thought to begin with. For nearly two years Gaza has been living through the events you know, and you know the condition of our people in Yemen and elsewhere, and what is happening in Sudan, and what do you know of Sudan, where the media blackout has reached a point at which no one can raise his phone to film a scene without being counted among the dead, as the news comes from the inside. So where is the condition of the Muslims? And what can we possibly do?

And this is a question we hear from time to time: we live in America, far from these events, and we have no part in them. So let us ask together: the Prophet ﷺ in Makkah, over thirteen years, was he in a state of fighting and physical defense of his religion, or in a state of struggle against the disbelief and polytheism surrounding him and his companions? What was the first concern of the caller in Makkah at that time?

### Makkah: the stage of faith-based preparation

The answer is that the Prophet ﷺ was forming people who carried faith in their hearts. And these individuals, few or many, moved from the stage of faith-based preparation in Makkah to Madinah to be soldiers for Allah who defend the religion with true defense, and they are the first wall of defense for the call and its people and for Islam and its followers. Makkah was a stage of faith-based preparation inside the souls, and so we saw Abu Bakr, ʿUmar, ʿUthmān and others whom Makkah brought forth pure in heart and mind. And that is what is required first: that faith inside you be a mover, and not a claim.

### The faith that is claimed and does not move

Listen to the words of Allah, the Mighty and Majestic: ﴿قَالَتِ الْأَعْرَابُ آمَنَّا ۖ قُل لَّمْ تُؤْمِنُوا وَلَٰكِن قُولُوا أَسْلَمْنَا وَلَمَّا يَدْخُلِ الْإِيمَانُ فِي قُلُوبِكُمْ﴾ [الحجرات: 14] (The Bedouins say, "We have believed." Say, "You have not [yet] believed; but say [instead], 'We have submitted,' for faith has not yet entered your hearts.") Have we tried to reflect on this wording? All of us claim faith on the individual level, and all of us know that truthfulness is salvation, but what is the level of lying in the lives of individuals and in the life of the Ummah? From all of us comes evil and good, error and right. But what error comes from me while I am certain of the opposite? I lie deliberately, I deceive deliberately, I bear false witness deliberately, I cheat in my selling and my trade deliberately, while I claim faith! I stand in the row of worshippers, I listen to the Qur'an, I recite pages, I repeat Sūrat al-Kahf on Friday, I hear the adhān, and all of that moves my body to the place of prayer so that I say: Allahu akbar, then I return to my trade and repeat the cheating, the deception, the forgery and the trickery. As if I go to the mosque to take a sedative for the conscience, then return with the very same body to do as I please, striving after this world which I hope for and hope for nothing beyond it.

So where is faith? All of us know that on the day we married we took the daughters of people by the trust of Allah and His word, so how many of us fear Allah regarding his household? And our women know, in theory as a matter of religion, that the man in the house is a trust with her, that he has rights over her and she has duties in her home, so which of them, whether many or few, fear Allah regarding their men and are good in companionship? And all of us know that our children in our homes are a trust that Allah deposited with us, and what is required of us is to prepare this generation to carry the identity of Islam and its message to tomorrow and to spread the religion of Allah across the corners of the earth. So which of us fears Allah regarding his dependents and gives them of his time more than he gives his friends and companions, and treats them well as much as he treats himself well? And which of us has set his mind on the Hereafter and strived for it with its due striving?

I do not want to speak at length today, for there is a great deal of talk here and there and in every place where people ascend the pulpits. But we are facing a crisis called the crisis of responding to what we speak about. The easiest thing we have is to wade into people's honor, to trace the footsteps of people, to obtain money by the forbidden or the doubtful, and the easiest thing we have is to betray with our eyes or our tongues or our bodies. So where is the faith whose very principle is that it should run through the body of the Muslim like blood in all his veins? This is a matter that needs review.

### Some seventy branches

Servants of Allah, we have no escape from faith. But silent faith moves neither mind nor body, and adulterated faith leads to what is worse than it. And the Prophet ﷺ tells us in a well-known ḥadīth: «الْإِيمَانُ بِضْعٌ وَسَبْعُونَ شُعْبَةً، فَأَفْضَلُهَا قَوْلُ لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَأَدْنَاهَا إِمَاطَةُ الْأَذَى عَنِ الطَّرِيقِ» (Faith is some seventy branches; the most excellent of them is the saying "There is no god but Allah," and the lowest of them is removing harm from the road).[^1] So what is between the most excellent and the lowest? What is between "There is no god but Allah" and "removing harm from the road"? Running the factories is among the requirements of faith, managing the shops is among the requirements of faith, the true upbringing of a sound generation is among the requirements of faith, and respectable political work is among the requirements of faith. All work is among the requirements of faith, so let us not belittle and not squander, but take into account what lies in our hands.

All of you know that Allah's victory for His believing servants is true, that whoever abandons the religion of Allah, Allah abandons him, and that whoever supports His message and His trust, the Lord of the worlds protects him. And all of us know His words: ﴿إِنَّا لَنَنصُرُ رُسُلَنَا وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا﴾ [غافر: 51] (Indeed, We will support Our messengers and those who believe), and that the soldiers of Allah are the victorious. This is how the Noble Qur'an has told us, and the matter is settled. So what is happening? What is going on inside people's souls? Why all this fear, this weakness, this servility? There is no reason except that faith does not move the hearts of these people.

The magicians of Pharaoh stood before Pharaoh, the greatest king of that time, so what was their expression when they announced their Islam at the highest level of announcement? They said: ﴿لَا ضَيْرَ ۖ إِنَّا إِلَىٰ رَبِّنَا مُنقَلِبُونَ﴾ [الشعراء: 50] (No harm. Indeed, to our Lord we will return). Their eyes turned away from their sanctified gaze at Pharaoh the ruler to the Lord of Might, Glorified is He, and they were transformed from servitude to a person, a worldly life, wealth, the dollar and the dinar, to servitude to the One who arranges the means, who breaks the means, and who does in His dominion whatever He wills.

I say this, and I seek Allah's forgiveness for myself and for you.

### Acts of worship are training in commitment

Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds, none can overturn His judgment and none can repel His decree, He is not questioned about what He does, but they are questioned, and I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, the King, the True and Manifest, and I bear witness that our master and our Prophet Muhammad is His servant, His prophet and His messenger, may Allah's blessings be upon him and upon his family and all his companions.

When Allah commanded people to come to the mosques five times in their day and night, He wanted to train them to commit to a work their hands make and a product that comes out from them. And when He commanded them to fast thirty consecutive days, He wanted to train the souls to refrain from what is not theirs, and that we not plunge into this world except with a faith that moves our bodies. And when He commanded them to perform Ḥajj, circling the Kaʿba and walking between the two mountains, He wanted us to return in our memory, our limbs and our qibla to the origin from which we came out, so that if one of us errs he returns, and if he disobeys he repents, going back and forth, but we do not leave the circle of Islam nor the circle of belonging to the Lord of Might, Glorified is He. Allah trains us to apply faith in our lives in every word and deed, and He asked of us an Islam that we perform and obligations that we keep, so keep to them, servants of Allah, and let time not be a proof against us before the Lord of the worlds on the Day of Resurrection. For if we stay up late at a table of cards or dominoes, we waste periods of time that could have been invested in what benefits the Ummah, develops minds in knowledge and action, raises offspring and moves an entire generation. And if we play with our words and squander the rights of others around us, we waste our own rights before the Lord of the worlds.

Servants of Allah, I remind myself first and before anyone else, and I remind my brothers and sisters: the life we live is a trust with which the Lord of the worlds entrusted us, and if we squander it we squander ourselves on the Day of Resurrection. And what is required today is one thing only: that we renew our sense that we are people of an identity, and the person of identity always tries to prove it, declare it and make it known among people. And what is beyond that will come. So let us not be liars in the claim of faith, for when we stand before Allah on the Day of Resurrection He will ask us about the truthfulness of what we claimed.

O Allah, support our people in Gaza and give them honor, strengthen their hearts, make their pillars firm and reinforce them with soldiers from Your soldiers, O Lord of the worlds. O Allah, prepare for the Ummah of Islam a matter of right guidance in which the people of obedience to You are honored, the people of disobedience to You are guided, and every stubborn tyrant is humbled. O Allah, guide us among those You have guided, and ease for us the means of obedience. And our final supplication is that praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
