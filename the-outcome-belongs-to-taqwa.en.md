> Source: A transcription of a video sermon by Dr. Ahmed Abouseif — [Watch the original video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zcIxsMWOIXY)

We praise Him, glory be to Him, and we seek His help, His guidance and His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah, Blessed and Exalted, from the evils of our souls and from our bad deeds. Whomever Allah guides, none can lead astray, and whomever He leads astray, none can guide. We bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner; His is the dominion and His is the praise, in His hand are the keys of the heavens and the earth, and He is able to do all things. And we bear witness that our master and our Prophet Muhammad is His servant, His prophet and His messenger ﷺ, and upon his family and companions and whoever walks his path until the Day of Judgment. To proceed:

It is not good for me to pass over what our shaykh kindly said before the Friday prayer about maintaining ties of kinship, for it falls within the title of our sermon: «And the outcome belongs to taqwā.» Maintaining kinship ties is part of an integrated body of legislation by which Allah willed to regulate people's lives and conduct, not with a grip that restrains movement, but with a method that keeps the journey on course. In the old days they taught us that my family is the safety valve of my life: my relatives, my maternal and paternal uncles, my cousins on both sides, surround me and I surround them.

### When people came to fear their relatives

But reality has worsened to the point where a person fears showing a blessing in front of his relatives and family, and we find ourselves advising our children: Don't talk in front of your aunt so-and-so, or mention such-and-such in front of your maternal uncle so-and-so, be careful, the house has its secrets. This is right in part, not in whole, for keeping secrets is commanded by the Sharia. And the question remains: how did we reach this level of rancor between relatives, and of envy between brothers of the same father and mother, from one womb and one loin?

The answer is that the absence of religiousness from souls is what drives people to walk behind Satan; these are his movements and his whisperings. This is why the first problem on the face of the earth was between two brothers: ﴿وَاتْلُ عَلَيْهِمْ نَبَأَ ابْنَيْ آدَمَ بِالْحَقِّ إِذْ قَرَّبَا قُرْبَانًا فَتُقُبِّلَ مِنْ أَحَدِهِمَا وَلَمْ يُتَقَبَّلْ مِنَ الْآخَرِ قَالَ لَأَقْتُلَنَّكَ ۖ قَالَ إِنَّمَا يَتَقَبَّلُ اللَّهُ مِنَ الْمُتَّقِينَ ۝ لَئِن بَسَطتَ إِلَيَّ يَدَكَ لِتَقْتُلَنِي مَا أَنَا بِبَاسِطٍ يَدِيَ إِلَيْكَ لِأَقْتُلَكَ ۖ إِنِّي أَخَافُ اللَّهَ رَبَّ الْعَالَمِينَ ۝ إِنِّي أُرِيدُ أَن تَبُوءَ بِإِثْمِي وَإِثْمِكَ فَتَكُونَ مِنْ أَصْحَابِ النَّارِ ۚ وَذَٰلِكَ جَزَاءُ الظَّالِمِينَ﴾ [المائدة: 27-29] (And recite to them the story of Adam's two sons, in truth, when they both offered a sacrifice [to Allah], and it was accepted from one of them but was not accepted from the other. Said [the latter], "I will surely kill you." Said [the former], "Indeed, Allah only accepts from the righteous [who fear Him]. If you should raise your hand against me to kill me - I shall not raise my hand against you to kill you. Indeed, I fear Allah, Lord of the worlds. Indeed I want you to obtain [thereby] my sin and your sin so you will be among the companions of the Fire. And that is the recompense of wrongdoers.") Here appeared the very same malady that had appeared earlier between Satan and Adam; Satan was able to spread his poisons into the soul of one of the two sons and not the other.

In the words of the wronged brother there is an integrated system of thought. First: how the reaction should be toward an evil that comes to me not from my cousin but from my brother. Second: a methodology of thinking about consequences and aftermaths, not about the event alone. Third: referring the whole matter back to Allah alone. And what did the other brother want? There are many reports, none of which is sound, but think of any reason you like, a woman he wanted to marry or money he wanted to take, and you will find it, from beginning to end, this world: numbers, calculations, cash, inheritance, land and a farm, all placed in a very small circle called the circle of this world.

### The circle of this world and the question "Why not me?"

In this circle people compete. If one of them sees a new shirt on you, he does not look at your heart but at your shirt, and if he sees that you have renovated something in your house, he looks at it. And this world passes away after a while. The wise person keeps repeating: ﴿إِنَّمَا يَتَقَبَّلُ اللَّهُ مِنَ الْمُتَّقِينَ﴾ [المائدة: 27] (Allah only accepts from the righteous), so he deals with Allah before anything and after anyone, and he does not look at people's actions; rather, he feels pity for them over the devil that has taken hold of their hearts and changed them from what they ought to be.

And I say this while feeling pain: when I need a supporter and a backer, it is my brother I want, who is from my father's loins or my mother's womb or with whom I am bound by the tie of religion. My brother, with whom I am not wronged while he is beside me, nor humbled while he raises my standing, nor impoverished while he has what is in his pocket because it is also mine; who raises my child if I die, guards my honor if I am away, grows my wealth if I neglect to look after it, and watches over my gear and equipment if my mind is occupied elsewhere. These notions have vanished from people's lives because the considerations of faith that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ established among people have vanished, so Satan became the mover of minds and hearts, and ideas came to follow the system he founded in the universe when he said for the first time: I am better than him. In the same way, everyone around you looks at what you have and says: Why not me? He is lesser than I am. He has a building, why him and not me? He has children excelling in their education and his life is stable, why? It is the same idea repeated in different words. And if we poured our effort into asking Allah to give us the like of what He gave others, we would succeed, and we would follow the way of the Prophet ﷺ.

### Mutual love and mercy like one body

The Prophet ﷺ taught people what brotherhood should be, as our shaykh reminded us of his ḥadīth: «The likeness of the believers in their mutual love, mercy and compassion is that of the body: when one limb complains, the rest of the body responds with sleeplessness and fever.»[^1] Memorize the two words: mutual love and mercy. In the Qur'an: ﴿وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً﴾ [الروم: 21] (and He placed between you affection and mercy), our Lord is speaking of the relationship between spouses, and the Messenger of Allah ﷺ speaks of the relationship between believers and says that it is love and mercy. As if he ﷺ were raising my relationship with you to the rank of my relationship with my household, to whom I cling night and day, with whom I eat, feel, think, grieve and rejoice. It is a relationship that joins not thought alone but thought and feelings, heart and pulse, bodily movement and movement of the mind, and it joins people with a joining after which there is no parting. And if parting occurs between two brothers, in the kinship of blood or the kinship of religion, then it is ﴿فَإِمْسَاكٌ بِمَعْرُوفٍ أَوْ تَسْرِيحٌ بِإِحْسَانٍ﴾ [البقرة: 229] (then either keep [her] in an acceptable manner or release [her] with good treatment), not quarrelling and feuding; they have no right, in the original legislation, to quarrel.

People compete for this world, and it is only a little provision that comes upon the servant and then leaves him after a while. Allah bestows blessings on whom He wills and takes them from whom He wills. Whoever looks to Allah and seeks what is with Him, his Lord fulfils him and gives him, and whoever deprives himself of connection with Allah is deprived of every blessing in this world, and even if he owned the wealth of Qārūn he would enjoy nothing, because the heart is empty.

Servants of Allah, I advise myself and you to have taqwā of Allah regarding family, children, mothers and fathers, paternal uncles and maternal uncles. For this world turns, and whoever does good today finds good done to him tomorrow, and if worldly goodness is not available to him, otherworldly goodness will be available to him, and the one who repays is the Lord of Might, glory be to Him.

I say this, and I seek Allah's forgiveness for myself and for you.

### What is the purpose for which we came?

Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds, and the outcome belongs to the righteous, and there is no hostility except against the wrongdoers. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, the Sovereign, the True, the Manifest, and I bear witness that our master and our Prophet Muhammad is His servant, His prophet and His messenger ﷺ, and upon his family and all his companions.

When our view narrowed to this world and what is in it, to numbers and counting them, to vaults and filling them, to banks and accounts, we lost sight of the basic goal for which we came into life and for which we will leave life held accountable. Our Lord said: ﴿وَإِذْ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلْمَلَائِكَةِ إِنِّي جَاعِلٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ خَلِيفَةً ۖ قَالُوا أَتَجْعَلُ فِيهَا مَن يُفْسِدُ فِيهَا وَيَسْفِكُ الدِّمَاءَ وَنَحْنُ نُسَبِّحُ بِحَمْدِكَ وَنُقَدِّسُ لَكَ ۖ قَالَ إِنِّي أَعْلَمُ مَا لَا تَعْلَمُونَ﴾ [البقرة: 30] (And [mention, O Muhammad], when your Lord said to the angels, "Indeed, I will make upon the earth a successive authority." They said, "Will You place upon it one who causes corruption therein and sheds blood, while we declare Your praise and sanctify You?" Allah said, "Indeed, I know that which you do not know."). In it Allah told us that He made you, made me and made each one of us a vicegerent who carries out what Allah wills. But we left this and turned to what we ourselves want, and we no longer looked outward and then inward, so the goal for which we are here was lost.

Let us take a simple example: if one of us comes from a far place, more than eighteen hours in the car, to attend this mosque, how could he not be early? How could he not be present and alert, listening to his shaykh attentively and respecting those sitting in front of him? Because this is his work, for which he came, and if he does not do it he is a failure. And we were sent into life so that each of us would be a vicegerent of Allah on His earth, so if we are absent from this we become failures. People have been absent from what Allah wanted of them and turned to what Satan wanted of them; they have been absent from the highest values and busied themselves with trivialities that exist on this earth.

### Yūsuf: he did not forget the purpose in prison

A very great injustice befell Yūsuf, peace be upon him; he was accused of a shameful charge and thrown into prison unjustly. How was his reaction? He did not forget the goal. When he entered prison he said to his two companions: ﴿يَا صَاحِبَيِ السِّجْنِ أَأَرْبَابٌ مُّتَفَرِّقُونَ خَيْرٌ أَمِ اللَّهُ الْوَاحِدُ الْقَهَّارُ﴾ [يوسف: 39] (O my two companions of prison, are separate lords better or Allah, the One, the Prevailing?). And when it was requested of him: ﴿أَفْتِنَا فِي سَبْعِ بَقَرَاتٍ سِمَانٍ﴾ [يوسف: 46] (Explain to us about seven fat cows), and he saw an opportunity to get out, he did not say: I am wronged here and want to get out, he did not say this at all, but said: ﴿تَزْرَعُونَ سَبْعَ سِنِينَ دَأَبًا﴾ [يوسف: 47] (You will plant for seven years consecutively). He did not forget his purpose: I am not in this world to be occupied with myself, but to develop a universe that Allah commanded me to develop, to spread a religion that Allah commanded me to spread, and to help people toward what is right. Whoever is absent from this purpose quarrels with his wife, feuds with his children, frowns at life and feels cramped in his condition, whereas whoever lives for Allah lives secure, settled, calm and at peace, tender toward people, giving what he has and giving to others.

### Abū Bakr and ʿUmar: the mindset of the believer

I will conclude with an example so as not to lengthen it for you. What mindset did Abū Bakr, may Allah be pleased with him, have as he gave the Prophet ﷺ everything he had? He was a very wealthy man, able to earn from his trade, in a short time, what others could not manage. Each time the Prophet ﷺ asked for a donation he would bring everything he had, and the Prophet would ask him: «What have you left for your family?» and he would say: I left them Allah and His Messenger.[^2] How was he thinking? He was thinking of the heavens, of Paradise, and that this might be the last gift he could give, and he wanted to obtain Paradise at the highest price, whatever the cost. If one of us were struck with a heart disease and were told: you need surgery that costs such-and-such, he would say: whatever the cost, I will spend everything I have to regain my health. Have you ever seen a sensible person who hoards money and does not want to save himself with anything? This was the theory of Abū Bakr al-Ṣiddīq.

And ʿUmar, may Allah be pleased with him, tried to compete once or twice, and brought half of his wealth, and when the Prophet ﷺ asked him what he had left them, he said: I left half of my wealth. So reflect: who is the winner, the successful one in worldly terms? The one who has half his wealth can grow his trade and increase his accounts, while the one who gave everything he had will start from zero. But it is the mindset of the believer.

O Allah, complete for our people in Gaza their affair with good, O Lord of the worlds. O Allah, do not let their effort go to waste, nor scatter their ranks, nor divide their word, nor split their line. O Allah, make them steadfast, help them and support them with Your support. O Allah, mend their hearts, guide their striving aright and bless their steps, and forgive us our shortcoming toward them and toward Your call and Your religion. O Allah, expand our chests for obedience to You and fill our hearts with certainty in what You have promised. O Allah, keep our homes safe for us from the scheming of Satan, and do not set over us anyone who does not fear You regarding us and has no mercy on us, and mend our hearts, guide our striving aright and bless our steps, O Lord of the worlds. And the last of our supplication is that praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds.
