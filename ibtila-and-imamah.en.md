> Occasion: A transcription of a lecture delivered in Woodbridge on 23 July 2026   
> Source: A transcription of a video lecture by Dr. Ahmed Abouseif — [Watch the original video on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nnu_9hh3NwA)

The Doctor opened with prayers and peace upon our master and Prophet Muhammad, and upon his family and Companions, with abundant peace. Then he made supplication: O Allah, we ask You for all good, its immediate and its delayed; and we seek refuge in You from all evil, its immediate and its delayed. We ask You for the things that necessitate Your mercy and the firm resolves of Your forgiveness, for a share of every righteous deed, and safety from every sin, and for the winning of Paradise and deliverance from the Fire. Our Lord, we ask You for the good of this night, the good of what is in it, and the good of what comes after it; and we seek refuge in You, our Lord, from the evil of this night, the evil of what is in it, and the evil of what comes after it. O Allah, amen, O Lord of the worlds.

Then he entered into Sūrat al-Baqarah, and prayed for good for those present.

### Vicegerency: Adam

He began from the verse of vicegerency: ﴿وَإِذْ قَالَ رَبُّكَ لِلْمَلَائِكَةِ إِنِّي جَاعِلٌ فِي الْأَرْضِ خَلِيفَةً﴾ [البقرة: 30] (And [mention] when your Lord said to the angels, "Indeed, I will make upon the earth a vicegerent"), and he mentioned Adam, peace be upon him, and repeated the word "vicegerent" (khalīfah).

### Leadership: Abraham

Then he moved on to Abraham, peace be upon him: «إني جاعلك للناس إماماً» ("I will make you a leader for the people"), thus joining the two words: the one made a vicegerent, and the leader (imām); vicegerency and leadership.[^1] And he recited: ﴿وَإِذِ ابْتَلَىٰ إِبْرَاهِيمَ رَبُّهُ بِكَلِمَاتٍ فَأَتَمَّهُنَّ …﴾ [البقرة] (And [mention] when Abraham was tried by his Lord with words [of command] and he fulfilled them …).[^4] He repeated "tried" (ibtalā), and said: leader, vicegerent, and repeated the name of Abraham, peace be upon him.

### Milestones in the Trials of Abraham

Then he went through milestones of his life. He mentioned Ishmael, peace be upon him, and he mentioned the desert and Makkah, and in his speech came an expression about "the womb of the mother" and "the womb of the desert."

Then he recited: ﴿أَلَمْ تَرَ إِلَى الَّذِي حَاجَّ إِبْرَاهِيمَ فِي رَبِّهِ أَنْ آتَاهُ اللَّهُ الْمُلْكَ إِذْ قَالَ إِبْرَاهِيمُ رَبِّيَ الَّذِي يُحْيِي وَيُمِيتُ قَالَ أَنَا أُحْيِي وَأُمِيتُ ۖ قَالَ إِبْرَاهِيمُ فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ يَأْتِي بِالشَّمْسِ مِنَ الْمَشْرِقِ فَأْتِ بِهَا مِنَ الْمَغْرِبِ فَبُهِتَ الَّذِي كَفَرَ ۗ وَاللَّهُ لَا يَهْدِي الْقَوْمَ الظَّالِمِينَ﴾ [البقرة: 258] (Have you not considered the one who argued with Abraham about his Lord [merely] because Allah had given him kingship? When Abraham said, "My Lord is the one who gives life and causes death," he said, "I give life and cause death." Abraham said, "Indeed, Allah brings up the sun from the east, so bring it up from the west." So the disbeliever was overwhelmed [by astonishment]; and Allah does not guide the wrongdoing people).

Then he spoke in English about one of the trials of his life, that he went to Egypt, and that someone there tried to take Sarah from him.[^2]

Then he mentioned the sacrifice, and recited: ﴿يَا بُنَيَّ إِنِّي أَرَىٰ فِي الْمَنَامِ أَنِّي أَذْبَحُكَ فَانظُرْ مَاذَا تَرَىٰ﴾ [الصافات: 102] ("O my son, indeed I see in a dream that I [must] sacrifice you, so see what you think"). Then he recited: ﴿إِنَّهُ مِنْ عِبَادِنَا الْمُؤْمِنِينَ﴾ ﴿إِنَّا كَذَٰلِكَ نَجْزِي الْمُحْسِنِينَ﴾ [الصافات] ("Indeed, he was of Our believing servants"; "Indeed, We thus reward the doers of good"),[^5] and after that he mentioned the trial.

### Trial Is a General Divine Way

Then he moved to Sūrat al-ʿAnkabūt, sought refuge in Allah from Satan the accursed, and recited: ﴿الم * أَحَسِبَ النَّاسُ أَن يُتْرَكُوا أَن يَقُولُوا آمَنَّا وَهُمْ لَا يُفْتَنُونَ﴾ [العنكبوت: 1-2] (Alif, Lām, Mīm. Do the people think that they will be left to say, "We believe," and they will not be tried?). Then he recited from Sūrat al-Mulk: ﴿تَبَارَكَ الَّذِي بِيَدِهِ الْمُلْكُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ * الَّذِي خَلَقَ الْمَوْتَ وَالْحَيَاةَ لِيَبْلُوَكُمْ أَيُّكُمْ أَحْسَنُ عَمَلًا ۚ وَهُوَ الْعَزِيزُ الْغَفُورُ * الَّذِي خَلَقَ سَبْعَ سَمَاوَاتٍ طِبَاقًا ۖ مَا تَرَىٰ فِي خَلْقِ … مِن تَفَاوُتٍ ۖ فَارْجِعِ الْبَصَرَ هَلْ …﴾,[^6] (Blessed is He in whose hand is dominion, and He is over all things competent; [He] who created death and life to test you [as to] which of you is best in deed; and He is the Exalted in Might, the Forgiving; [He] who created seven heavens in layers. You do not see in the creation … any disparity; so return your vision [to the sky]; do you see any …), and he repeated the word "to test you" (liyabluwakum) and mentioned along with it the trial of Abraham.

### What Is Inherited and What Passes Away

After that he spoke in English, apparently about brief glimpses of the life of one person here and another there, and that those people's lives have passed; they were trying to gather wealth, and through position, and through everything. Then he spoke about the inheritance we receive from our fathers: where did they themselves get it? Perhaps from their fathers, and those, where did they get it from? Perhaps from others. The land is the land, the building is the building, the money is the money, and the bank account is the bank account.

### Trial in Our Lives, and the List of Prophets

Then he said in English that, when facing any suffering in his life, with people, or in money, or at home and in the family, he asks himself "one question," and then he enumerated prophets: Abraham, Noah, Moses, Joseph, Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, Jesus, David, Solomon, Zechariah, Elias, John, Jonah, peace be upon them. After that came English words: "You can go to any country… do not think that you can get out of…".

### The Message of Allah and the Way of Replacement

Then he recited: ﴿اللَّهُ أَعْلَمُ حَيْثُ يَجْعَلُ رِسَالَتَهُ﴾ (Allah is most knowing of where [i.e., with whom] He places His message), and he recited: ﴿وَإِن تَتَوَلَّوْا يَسْتَبْدِلْ قَوْمًا غَيْرَكُمْ ثُمَّ لَا يَكُونُوا أَمْثَالَكُم﴾ (And if you turn away, He will replace you with another people; then they will not be the likes of you),[^7] and after the two he mentioned "the All-Powerful" and "Allah, Exalted and Majestic, alone."

### The Human Being Between Honor and Humiliation

Then he mentioned the trial again, and recited: ﴿فَأَمَّا الْإِنسَانُ إِذَا مَا ابْتَلَاهُ رَبُّهُ فَأَكْرَمَهُ وَنَعَّمَهُ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّي أَكْرَمَنِ * وَأَمَّا إِذَا مَا ابْتَلَاهُ فَقَدَرَ عَلَيْهِ رِزْقَهُ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّي أَهَانَنِ * كَلَّا ۖ بَل لَّا تُكْرِمُونَ الْيَتِيمَ﴾ (As for man, when his Lord tries him and honors him and favors him, he says, "My Lord has honored me." But when He tries him and restricts his provision, he says, "My Lord has humiliated me." No! Rather, you do not honor the orphan).[^3]

### Raising the House, and Vicegerency and Leadership

Then he mentioned Ishmael, peace and blessings be upon him, and Abraham as he "raises from the House," and he mentioned the trial. In an English comment he mentioned that some people come to him and say such-and-such to him.

He concluded by returning to the central theme: vicegerent, vicegerency, and he mentioned Muhammad ibn ʿAbd Allāh ﷺ, and Adam, peace be upon him, and Abraham, peace be upon him, then: vicegerency, and leader (imām), and he repeated "praise be to Allah" and mentioned the Qur'an, and the lecture ended with "O Allah, amen, O Lord of the worlds."

[^2]: Editor's documentation: The verse recited before it is al-Baqarah: 258 (the editor's attribution), and the lecturer did not state its connection to what follows.
[^3]: Editor's documentation: The verses are from Sūrat al-Fajr: 15-17 (the editor's attribution; the lecturer did not name the sūrah). The correct reading in the verse is «لا تكرمون», which is what has been set down.
[^4]: Editor's documentation: The completion of the verse (al-Baqarah: 124): ﴿قَالَ إِنِّي جَاعِلُكَ لِلنَّاسِ إِمَامًا﴾ ("He said, 'Indeed, I will make you a leader for the people'"), and the lecturer had mentioned its meaning before it, using the words "khalīfah" and "imām."
[^5]: Editor's documentation: The lecturer uttered the two verses in this order, whereas their order in the muṣḥaf, al-Ṣāffāt: 110 is «إنا كذلك نجزي المحسنين» and then 111 «إنه من عبادنا المؤمنين».
[^6]: Editor's documentation: The verses are from Sūrat al-Mulk: 1-3 (the editor's attribution). The verse reads «ما ترى في خلق الرحمن من تفاوت».
[^7]: Editor's documentation: The two verses: al-Anʿām: 124, and Muḥammad: 38 (the editor's attribution; the lecturer did not name the two sūrahs).
