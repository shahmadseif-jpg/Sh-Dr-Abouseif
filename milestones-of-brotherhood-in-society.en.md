Praise be to Allah, we praise Him, seek His help, seek His guidance and seek His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah from the evils of our souls and from the wrongs of our deeds, and we bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and that Muhammad is His servant and His messenger ﷺ. I counsel you and myself to fear Allah and to be mindful of Him in secret and in public. To proceed:

Imagine a single house that brings together a man and a woman on the covenant that Allah took from them on the day they married. And in the breast of each there is a grudge against the other that is years old: stances that piled up, souls that boil, minds that do not stop reckoning, and an accursed devil leading this whole procession, who leaves them no chance to notice a single word, "pardon."

Everyone knows this word on their tongues, and remembers it whenever they need it from others. But when the turn of giving comes, stinginess appears and concepts in the mind change: I do not give as much as I take, indeed I do not give at all. And so our talk today is about one of the foundations of society.

### The first thing built in the society of Madina

When the Prophet ﷺ entered Madina as an emigrant, before he did anything else, he made brotherhood between the Muhājirūn and the Anṣār.[^1] He knew that the matter of Islam in the whole earth would be established through this nascent society, so if brotherhood was among the first bricks in building the Muslims, we know its value.

On what basis, then, were the milestones of brotherhood among the members of that society established? If I want to clear things between two people, my talk is about pardon: ﴿وَأَن تَعْفُوا أَقْرَبُ لِلتَّقْوَىٰ﴾ [البقرة: 237] (And to forego it is nearer to righteousness). This is our motto on this blessed day. And does a society stand without this meaning? I say it briefly: pardon is the safety valve of the home, and the safety valve of society.

Tell me: if you are a physician, how do I surrender my body to you while in my soul there is something against you? The heart must be sound and the soul at ease for me to rely on your advice. And how do I receive knowledge from a teacher I cannot bear to look at and cannot tolerate what comes from his tongue? And if we are neighbors, every sunrise is a new worry because I will be forced to see him. And if we are spouses in one house, then day and night are regret for us, and we seek refuge in Allah.

### What is pardon?

Pardon is that you pass the matter on to something else and do not look back. That you recognize another's error and then pass over it with your mind and your soul. Do not stop at every small and large thing; count it, and Allah will be pleased with you.

And the Qur'an brought together in one place pardon, overlooking and forgiveness. That came after the words of the Exalted: ﴿يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِنَّ مِنْ أَزْوَاجِكُمْ وَأَوْلَادِكُمْ عَدُوًّا لَّكُمْ فَاحْذَرُوهُمْ﴾ (O you who have believed, indeed, among your wives and your children are enemies to you, so beware of them), and the apparent sense of the speech up to there is that companionship becomes impossible, dealings are cut off, ties are scattered, and each goes to his own valley. Then the verse follows: ﴿وَإِن تَعْفُوا وَتَصْفَحُوا وَتَغْفِرُوا فَإِنَّ اللَّهَ غَفُورٌ رَّحِيمٌ﴾ [التغابن: 14] (But if you pardon and overlook and forgive - then indeed, Allah is Forgiving and Merciful). And so the matter can be treated. As if Allah gathers all that for you: that you pardon with yourself, overlook with your mind, and forgive with both together, and life sets out anew as if nothing had been.

### Three places, three degrees

And we proceed with the Qur'an in its order.

In Sūrat al-Baqara Allah says: ﴿وَدَّ كَثِيرٌ مِّنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَابِ لَوْ يَرُدُّونَكُم مِّن بَعْدِ إِيمَانِكُمْ كُفَّارًا حَسَدًا مِّنْ عِندِ أَنفُسِهِم مِّن بَعْدِ مَا تَبَيَّنَ لَهُمُ الْحَقُّ ۖ فَاعْفُوا وَاصْفَحُوا حَتَّىٰ يَأْتِيَ اللَّهُ بِأَمْرِهِ﴾ [البقرة: 109] (Many of the People of the Scripture wish they could turn you back to disbelief after you have believed, out of envy from themselves [even] after the truth has become clear to them. So pardon and overlook until Allah delivers His command). A great crime: a people lying in wait for you night and day, checking the places of weakness to enter through them. And yet the command came directly: ﴿فَاعْفُوا وَاصْفَحُوا﴾ (So pardon and overlook). And Allah's command that we are waiting for may be one you are a cause of; when you pardon and treat well, and set yourself right before people before you set yourself right before yourself, so they see in you a good model and a fine example, and then they declare their Islam in your presence when they had been your enemies. So the pardon of passing over here is because you have a greater aim: that these people become Muslims. You lower your gaze from what comes from them out of compassion for them, for they do not yet realize the value of the word "creed," nor the meaning of "sharia," nor the reality of "conveying the message."

It is like the skilled merchant with a customer who has worn him out, he is patient with him and overlooks everything until he puts his signature, and the deal is concluded. So what of one who is patient to win a soul for Islam?

And in Sūrat Āl ʿImrān: ﴿وَسَارِعُوا إِلَىٰ مَغْفِرَةٍ مِّن رَّبِّكُمْ وَجَنَّةٍ عَرْضُهَا السَّمَاوَاتُ وَالْأَرْضُ أُعِدَّتْ لِلْمُتَّقِينَ ۝ الَّذِينَ يُنفِقُونَ فِي السَّرَّاءِ وَالضَّرَّاءِ وَالْكَاظِمِينَ الْغَيْظَ وَالْعَافِينَ عَنِ النَّاسِ ۗ وَاللَّهُ يُحِبُّ الْمُحْسِنِينَ﴾ [آل عمران: 133–134] (And hasten to forgiveness from your Lord and a garden as wide as the heavens and earth, prepared for the righteous. Who spend [in the cause of Allah] during ease and hardship and who restrain anger and who pardon the people - and Allah loves the doers of good). Where do we place pardon here, with righteousness and its people or with good-doing and its people? It falls in the space between the two. What is meant is that your pardon of people's errors and transgressions is not an exceptional matter in your life, but a goal; just as your reaching the mosque to pray is a goal. And as you gain the good deed here, you gain good-doing there. And between a prostration in which we learn humility and lower the wing to the Lord of Might, and pardon of creation, the meaning is completed. And the one who soars in the sky does not want to hold on to anything on the earth. And so with the people of pardon: their souls are lofty, their chests are raised, they do not descend to the level of those who comment on the behavior of fools. You hear them say: we are above that.

Then also in Āl ʿImrān our Lord says to His Prophet ﷺ: ﴿فَبِمَا رَحْمَةٍ مِّنَ اللَّهِ لِنتَ لَهُمْ ۖ وَلَوْ كُنتَ فَظًّا غَلِيظَ الْقَلْبِ لَانفَضُّوا مِنْ حَوْلِكَ ۖ فَاعْفُ عَنْهُمْ وَاسْتَغْفِرْ لَهُمْ وَشَاوِرْهُمْ فِي الْأَمْرِ﴾ [آل عمران: 159] (So by mercy from Allah, [O Muhammad], you were lenient with them. And if you had been rude [in speech] and harsh in heart, they would have disbanded from about you. So pardon them and ask forgiveness for them and consult them in the matter). A command of obligation: pardon them, and it is not enough to be silent and keep their news in your head, but remove them from your memory, and ask forgiveness for them. The verse was revealed concerning the events of Uḥud, but the lesson is in the generality of the wording and not the specificity of the cause. So the guidance is fixed in its place: in al-Baqara it is passing over people's errors even if they are great; in Āl ʿImrān it is a character trait that the people of righteousness are described with; and in the second verse a quality to which only the prophets and those who embody the state of the prophets rise. And see how the command to consult came after pardon and seeking forgiveness, for when can you sit with a person and consult with him unless the soul is pure and free of sediments and grudges?

And those who say they want to apply the Sunna: where are you from his character ﷺ? Do not let his guidance pass over you in passing.

### Even with the treacherous

And from Sūrat al-Māʾida: ﴿فَبِمَا نَقْضِهِم مِّيثَاقَهُمْ لَعَنَّاهُمْ وَجَعَلْنَا قُلُوبَهُمْ قَاسِيَةً ۖ يُحَرِّفُونَ الْكَلِمَ عَن مَّوَاضِعِهِ وَنَسُوا حَظًّا مِّمَّا ذُكِّرُوا بِهِ ۚ وَلَا تَزَالُ تَطَّلِعُ عَلَىٰ خَائِنَةٍ مِّنْهُمْ إِلَّا قَلِيلًا مِّنْهُمْ ۖ فَاعْفُ عَنْهُمْ وَاصْفَحْ ۚ إِنَّ اللَّهَ يُحِبُّ الْمُحْسِنِينَ﴾ [المائدة: 13] (So for their breaking of the covenant We cursed them and made their hearts hard. They distort words from their [proper] usages and have forgotten a portion of that of which they were reminded. And you will still observe deceit among them, except a few of them. But pardon them and overlook [their misdeeds]. Indeed, Allah loves the doers of good). If only the matter had stopped at disbelief and altering words, but they do not cease from treachery. And yet the divine command comes: ﴿فَاعْفُ عَنْهُمْ وَاصْفَحْ﴾ (But pardon them and overlook). And here again pardon is joined with good-doing, as if you deal with people from a very high level, even with those who betray Allah and His Messenger.

And you are in origin a caller. Do not say that calling is limited to the shaykhs and the masters of the pulpits; you are a caller in your homeland, in your clothing, in your work, and in your dealings with people wherever you are. So if an error occurs from another, it should not occur from you.

### When the blow comes from a relative

A blow, when it comes from a stranger, is made light of: what is my connection with him? We have known each other for a month or two, and he did to me a stance or two, so let us pass over it. But when it comes from a relative it is severe.

So listen to the words of Yūsuf to his brothers: ﴿لَا تَثْرِيبَ عَلَيْكُمُ الْيَوْمَ ۖ يَغْفِرُ اللَّهُ لَكُمْ ۖ وَهُوَ أَرْحَمُ الرَّاحِمِينَ﴾ [يوسف: 92] (No blame will there be upon you today. Allah will forgive you; and He is the most merciful of the merciful). What did these brothers do? They wanted to kill their brother, and had Allah not arranged another matter he would have been dead. And the more wondrous is that the brother pardons, so what of the father? Imagine that your children are before you, and one of them wants to kill his brother, as if he were killing you. Then they say: ﴿يَا أَبَانَا اسْتَغْفِرْ لَنَا ذُنُوبَنَا إِنَّا كُنَّا خَاطِئِينَ﴾ (O our father, ask for us forgiveness of our sins; indeed, we have been sinners), so how was the answer? It is the language of the great: ﴿قَالَ سَوْفَ أَسْتَغْفِرُ لَكُمْ رَبِّي ۖ إِنَّهُ هُوَ الْغَفُورُ الرَّحِيمُ﴾ [يوسف: 97–98] (He said, "I will ask forgiveness for you from my Lord. Indeed, it is He who is the Forgiving, the Merciful."). And matters ended in their place.

So if your wife apologizes to you once, the command has been carried out and the matter is over. And if your husband mends your heart, likewise. And we do not hold each other to account every day with the history of what passed between us in twenty-five or thirty years of marriage, recalling matters in their details. Have mercy on us a little, sisters, and you men likewise. Make pardon a name and a character and a mentality and a personality, and a way of dealing among people. And your friend who played a trick on you twenty years ago and you do not want to speak to him: count it as gone, man, the days and nights have passed.

And look at the harm the Prophet ﷺ met in Makkah, which was indescribable. Then came the day of the Conquest, and his famous sentence is narrated to its people: "Go, for you are the freed."[^2] The matter ended. And the son of the greatest enemy of Islam, ʿIkrima ibn Abī Jahl, his wife came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: O Messenger of Allah, ask for security for ʿIkrima, for he has set out to take to the sea. He said: "I have given him security." No dialogue and no debate; and he did not put the matter into the political security room. And ʿIkrima came afterward, and he ﷺ said to his Companions: "ʿIkrima will come to you as a delegate, so do not curse his father, for cursing the dead harms the living and does not reach the dead." See how intellect is embodied in his words ﷺ, my father and mother be ransom for him. So the people held their tongues. And the narration says that he ﷺ rose to him with his arms open saying: "Welcome to the rider, the emigrant."[^3] As if he were saying: where are you going? Between us is kinship, between us is blood, between us is language and humanity, between us is a prayer on which we gathered. And among the harshest kinds of rifts is that they be between worshippers in one mosque, or between worshippers of one Lord.

### The scale with Allah

When shall we realize that errors in people are compounded, and that they are part of human constitution? And that the origin in us is deficiency, not perfection? As much as you want people to pardon you, they want you to pardon them. And all of us seek Allah's pardon, and whoever does not do well in pardoning Allah's creation, how does he seek pardon from the Lord of creation? The equations are extremely easy, but we magnify matters, especially small things inside homes. One of us insists on sleeping vexed, so he brings in the small matter and magnifies it, and his family is harmed along with him.

Then we say to ourselves: every day you can pass with a sound heart, do not muddy its clarity. And every night you can spend at ease from a quarrel with your husband, do a kindness to yourself and to him, and do not let the children wake up vexed. And every moment that passes over a person while he is safe from thinking about another's scheme against him is a paradise that the people of faith live and the people of grudge and hatred do not know.

And look at what has happened in our society: this one speaks of his uncle, this one of his maternal uncle, this one of his relative. Our minds stopped thinking about the real priorities and were occupied with trivia: we talk about so-and-so, accuse so-and-so, and diminish the right of so-and-so. And if we had a work and a goal and looked to the future, we would not turn to these matters.

And as much as you love people to call you "ustādh" and "doctor" and "respected sir," you must be equal to these titles. So what if the title is "O believer"? You must be at the level of the title. And if you wish to rise, be among the people of good-doing, "O muḥsin," but it is a title that none will call you by except the angels, by command of the Lord of the worlds. So look when an angel calls you one of the doers of good. And the angels ﴿يَسْتَغْفِرُونَ لِلَّذِينَ آمَنُوا رَبَّنَا وَسِعْتَ كُلَّ شَيْءٍ رَّحْمَةً وَعِلْمًا فَاغْفِرْ لِلَّذِينَ تَابُوا وَاتَّبَعُوا سَبِيلَكَ وَقِهِمْ عَذَابَ الْجَحِيمِ﴾ [غافر: 7] (and ask forgiveness for those who have believed, [saying], "Our Lord, You have encompassed all things in mercy and knowledge, so forgive those who have repented and followed Your way and protect them from the punishment of Hellfire."), and they know our conditions better than we know one another.

I say this, and I seek Allah's forgiveness for myself and for you.

### The supplication

O Allah, make permanent the harmony of our hearts, remove the rancor from our breasts, bring together the heads of households and those in need, and make us among those who love one another for Your sake. O Allah, grant us soundness of mind, good understanding of situations, good dealing, and consideration of the consequences between us and our families and brothers. O Allah, make us among those who will say one day: ﴿إِنَّا كُنَّا قَبْلُ فِي أَهْلِنَا مُشْفِقِينَ ۝ فَمَنَّ اللَّهُ عَلَيْنَا وَوَقَانَا عَذَابَ السَّمُومِ﴾ [الطور: 26–27] (They will say, "Indeed, we were previously among our people fearful [of displeasing Allah]. So Allah conferred favor upon us and protected us from the punishment of the Scorching Fire."). O Allah, be pleased with us and pass over our errors and evil deeds, have mercy on our weakness, pardon our slips, take charge of our affair, and mend our brokenness, O Most Merciful of the merciful.
