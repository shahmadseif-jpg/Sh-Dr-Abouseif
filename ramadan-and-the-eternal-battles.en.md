Listen to the voices of the students of the Noble Qurʾān now. They are voices filling a place that would not have stood had it not been for a brother of ours, a shaykh, may Allah have mercy on him with a vast mercy, who was a cause of its existence and the existence of those in it. So the first thing we begin with today is a renewed supplication for him, that Allah write this place in the scale of his good deeds.

All praise is due to Allah; we praise Him, seek His help, seek His guidance and seek His forgiveness, and we seek refuge in Allah from the evils of our own selves and from our misdeeds, and we bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, with no partner, and that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger ﷺ. To proceed:

How happy is the human being when he leaves behind him a trace of the remembrance of Allah, and how great is his loss if he is deceived by the world and stores up from it what he leaves behind his back, which another enjoys while he is held to account for it. Glory be to the One who apportioned provisions and understandings among the creatures, and made for every human being a guidance and a path to walk in, and apportioned feelings among people, so one of them feels his nearness to the Lord of the worlds, and another feels his nearness to Satan, and we seek refuge in Allah.

### Half has passed and half remains

Servants of Allah, half of Ramadan has passed and the second half has begun, and what remains will not be like what has passed. And if the one with a plan had from the beginning work equal to the time, then the time has passed and the work remains: completed or not completed, and Allah knows best. And what is still required of each of us is to double the effort. And why not? For the days have come as ﴿أَيَّامًا مَّعْدُودَاتٍ﴾ [البقرة: 184] (a limited number of days) as our Lord named them, in proportion to what is required of the month in which ﴿أُنزِلَ فِيهِ الْقُرْآنُ﴾ [البقرة: 185] (was revealed the Qurʾān). Allah made it a complete month: if something is taken from it, something is taken from the effect that follows from it, and if the servant completes the work in it he has accomplished what Allah wanted from it.

So how do you feel now? Do you see in yourself a kind of connection with Allah, or has the self stayed in its place as it was before Ramadan came? Has your mind awakened to the system of realities to which we should take refuge, or have we stayed where we were, unchanged and unaltered? And do not be surprised at this saying, for among us is one who has spent sixty or seventy or fifty years of his life, and his conditions are still as they are and his traits fixed on their beginning, not wanting to change for the better, nor to develop himself, nor to reach an aim in the presence of the Lord of Might.

### Ṭufayl ibn ʿAmr: a man who blocked his ears and so listened

And I set beside people like these a man who entered Mecca one day amid the resounding warnings of those who counted themselves the protectors of the world at that time, such as Abū Jahl and Abū Lahab. They stopped him at the gate of Mecca and warned him of that man who separates a man from his wife and a father from his son. And it reached the point that he put cotton in his ears, kursuf as the narration says, warning himself with every warning against hearing a single word from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.

And as he was circling the Kaʿba in the circling of the Jāhiliyya and its people, he talked to himself and said: Woe to you, Ṭufayl! You are a sensible man, who knows right from wrong and the precious from the worthless, so why do you not listen to the man and see what he has? If what he has is right, you will be with him, and if it is otherwise, you will have caught up with him while you are the discerning and intelligent one. So he went to Muhammad ﷺ where he was, and sat before him, and the Prophet ﷺ began to recite to him. And at once, with an immediacy that admits no postponement at all, he says: I bear witness that there is no god but Allah, and that you are the Messenger of Allah.

Then what did he do? All he did was to say: O Messenger of Allah, among Daws adultery has spread, so send me to them as your messenger, O Messenger of Allah. A man whose aspiration carried him to win and to move with the speed of lightning toward the fullest goal for him, wanting to attain Paradise in the quickest time, for he does not know whether he will remain until tomorrow or not.

And look at us. What is there after Islam with us? Sitting beside the wall waiting for the prayer to be established, and when it is established we are at a degree of boredom according to the length of the recitation or the beauty of the performance and the voice. Then what after that? If a door to Paradise is offered to us in charity or otherwise, we review ourselves and look at the needs, and at what the woman will say if I return home and say: I gave in charity. And behold, it is an unbearable punishment! I am at ease from it now. If a door of good is offered to me I postpone it, and I put the needs of my body, my self and my family ahead of the needs of the Muslim community and the tools for running it as it should be. Then what after that? Nothing more than plenty of food and manifold desire and many-sided talk, nothing more than a world that passes.

### He witnessed Ramadan

The Messenger of Allah ﷺ tells us an engrossing model: two men between whom there was deep companionship. One of them was killed in a battle, so the people called him a martyr, and logic and reality are with them. And his companion died after him by a year, and one of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ saw them in a dream, and behold, the one who died later had preceded to the gate of Paradise! He was astonished, and woke, and returned to the Prophet ﷺ asking: O Messenger of Allah, the first died before our eyes in the battle as a martyr, then the second died after him by a year, so how does he enter Paradise before him? And the answer came with what had not crossed the mind of anyone who was nor of those who come after them: Did he not live a year after him? And in the narration he reached Ramadan and fasted it.[^1] It is as though witnessing Ramadan equals striving on the field of battle.

Yes, Ramadan has come to reshape the human self, to remold this human mentality, and to rearrange the ambitions in you, O believer who seeks the pleasure of the Lord of Might. Many of us do not reflect that Ramadan is the ladder of ascent to Allah, Blessed and Exalted. And for that reason the verse of supplication was set in the midst of the verses of fasting:

﴿وَإِذَا سَأَلَكَ عِبَادِي عَنِّي فَإِنِّي قَرِيبٌ ۖ أُجِيبُ دَعْوَةَ الدَّاعِ إِذَا دَعَانِ﴾ [البقرة: 186] (And when My servants ask you, [O Muhammad], concerning Me - indeed I am near. I respond to the invocation of the supplicant when he calls upon Me.)

It is as though the accepted supplication has its most fitting stations in the middle of these blessed days, so there is nothing more hopeful for supplication than these times.

### Eternal battles

We are before eternal battles from the beginning to the end. Whoever thinks he has escaped them has perished in his reason and thought, and whoever takes them up stays in constant alertness and complete wakefulness, his aspiration not weakening for a moment nor his resolve slackening for a second. A man awake and a woman whose aspiration is ablaze, why? Because the enmity is ancient, standing since the first creation:

﴿فَوَسْوَسَ إِلَيْهِ الشَّيْطَانُ قَالَ يَا آدَمُ هَلْ أَدُلُّكَ عَلَىٰ شَجَرَةِ الْخُلْدِ وَمُلْكٍ لَّا يَبْلَىٰ﴾ [طه: 120] (Satan whispered to him; he said, "O Adam, shall I direct you to the tree of eternity and possession that will not deteriorate?")

These are two symbols of two desires that will remain in human offspring until the Day of Resurrection, the two that possess the human being most. One of them is the love of immortality, whose form is the inclination of each of the two sexes to the other, the shared desire that stands until the Day of Resurrection, and what follows from it among women of displaying themselves or departing from the original and the familiar, and among men of craving or looking at the forbidden. And the other is the love of possession, and what accompanies it of theft, cheating, forgery, false testimony, working in falsehood and consuming people's wealth unjustly, to the last of the twists after which the human being thinks he has gained a goal.

That was the beginning. Then the Lord of the worlds shows us that there is an enemy lying in wait for you, but this time from the depths of your inside:

﴿إِنَّ النَّفْسَ لَأَمَّارَةٌ بِالسُّوءِ إِلَّا مَا رَحِمَ رَبِّي﴾ [يوسف: 53] (Indeed, the soul is a persistent enjoiner of evil, except those upon which my Lord has mercy.)

And whom does Allah have mercy on except the one who sought His mercy and stepped toward it with his feet, indeed with his aspiration, mind and heart. We strive for that and give for its sake, otherwise we are liars, and we seek refuge in Allah from that. Stepping toward Allah is not a light matter, but is by giving the dear and the cheap, and by giving lifetimes. And that appeared to us in the scenes of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ.

For here is a man standing on the thresholds of the battle, who asks himself a question of utmost simplicity: Is there anything between me and Paradise except my eating these dates? Imagine the scene with me: the army is preparing for an imminent assault, the arrows will come from every direction, and the man is strengthening his body with a few dates in his hand. Not a lunch, nor roast meat, nor preparation, just dates soiled with dust. Then he says to himself: By Allah, that would be a long life! So he throws them and sets off.[^2] What carried him to that? Rather the fuller question: What delays us from that?

We are not now before sensory battles in which we carry weapons while we sit in this place. Rather we have battles with our selves that prefer the worldly life over the Hereafter, and favor the enjoyments of bodies over the enjoyments of spirits, and purify the limbs more than they purify hearts and feelings. Then there are those around us: people bad in their character who lead some of us to respond with the like of what they do, which is contrary to our character. And there are those who stir up indecency among those around us while we look on, so either we take a lesson or we plunge in, and we seek refuge in Allah from distance from Him.

### The Qurʾān teaches us that the world is dunyā

The Qurʾān is the delight of time, the outing of the spirits, and the meadows of Paradise recited before you. In it Allah displays to us the lives of the earlier and the later peoples, and has us reassess our selves by looking in its pages, reading its verses and taking a lesson from its words. And it is Allah's greatest blessing upon all creation.

And it teaches us in great simplicity that the worldly life is merely dunyā (the lower), and that whoever wants to rise must leave it for what is higher. If we understand that, we look at the biggest house, the greatest company, the most precious product and the widest land, and at life with all its numbers, buildings and savings, as a lower world that we see when we look down and not up. And the Qurʾān taught us that what will leave the world is not the body that you care for night and day, clothe in the best that is fitting, clean from time to time, and desire to be soft and supple. This body is the thing that will remain under the soil. And there is a self, there is a spirit that will ascend one day, so either it goes out to Allah with the radiance of bliss in it, or goes out wretched and pale with no trace in it. And the Qurʾān taught us that the description of Paradise is the greatest thing described, for my eye before my heart.

### Ḥanẓala and his hypocrisy

And in a moment of self-review one of the Companions of the Prophet ﷺ asks himself: How far have I reached in hypocrisy? So he goes out from his house not knowing where to set off, and meets Abū Bakr, may Allah be pleased with him. He says to him: I have become a hypocrite, O Abū Bakr! He says: What is wrong with you, man? He says: We are with the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and he tells us about Paradise and the Fire as if they were before our eyes, we live the state and feel it, we enjoy the Paradise that is described and fear the Fire that is warned of. Then when we return to our homes we play with the children, romp with the wives and look to the estates. He does nothing forbidden, but he counted this temporary departure from the feeling of the Hereafter a kind of hypocrisy.

So Abū Bakr listens to his words and reflects on them, and says: By Allah, all of us are that man, so if this is hypocrisy then we are equal in the accusation. Come with us to the scout who does not lie to his people. So the two set off to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, and the man cannot hold his words and repeats: Ḥanẓala has become a hypocrite, Ḥanẓala has become a hypocrite, O Messenger of Allah! So the Prophet ﷺ calms his alarm: Sit, speak, what is the matter with you? And he said to him in his answer:

«By the One in whose hand is my soul, if you were to remain as you are with me and in remembrance, the angels would shake hands with you on your beds and in your roads; but, O Ḥanẓala, an hour and an hour.»[^3]

The answer of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ came to increase this man's reassurance and calm his alarm. For the station in which you are with him, of witnessing Paradise and the Fire and this feeling, is a precious feeling. And we feel it only a little.

And I wonder, and I ask myself before anyone: when was the last time you felt the sweetness of a prostration, and lengthened the prostration because it was hard for you to part with it, and difficult for you to raise your head from the ground? When was the last time you felt you were absorbed in glorification to the degree that you saw your spirit connecting to the heavens in a direct connection? When was the last time you enjoyed a verse of the Qurʾān so that you kept repeating it because it was like a melody that delights the heart and mind, so you lived it as a life and enjoyed it? Then we do not find it strange of those we used to hear of that he would repeat a single verse for a whole night in his prayer. A single verse! It is the pleasure of faith, O beloved of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.

### What remains of life

Servants of Allah, in what remains to us of time, and I do not mean Ramadan alone, for Ramadan is only a part of the age in which we pass and it passes over us, we want to determine truly the goal of our life, and to draw for ourselves a wise plan by which we cross this world to the Hereafter. That brother has gone to his Lord and moved from the first abode to the other abode, and many tongues remain repeating supplication for him. So who among us stands in a station like his? And he is only one of the righteous individuals of the Ummah, and before us were those who preceded to more good and whose aspiration rose with him to what is higher, purer and better. And to Allah is the praise for what He gave and to Him is the praise for what He decreed for us.

And I have come to you today again intending the support of Allah the Exalted through you. We have an institute for preparing callers and building new imams, here beside you in Sachse. The American Imams Academy specializes in making this generation that carries the concern of the call, and stands in a station like this and speaks to people with words like these. That is our goal and this is our project.

O Allah, return us to You a gracious return. O Allah, protect the blood of the Muslims in every land of the earth, and make us firm with the firm word in the worldly life and in the Hereafter. O Allah, do not make a sitting back take the place of obedience to You for us, and do not make an aspiration sit back from work in Your way. O Allah, make us a support for Your religion in these times and places.
